## Surfer SEO (PartnerStack · `surferseo` · `tier1starter`)

Eingereicht über `dash.partnerstack.com`. Surfer ist ein Content-/SEO-Optimierungs-Tool —
passt direkt zur eigenen programmatischen-SEO-Praxis und zur Zielgruppe.

**What affiliate experience do you have?**

> I run aban news, a German-language media brand for AI and productivity tools aimed at DACH professionals. It includes a daily newsletter and an AI-tools comparison directory (radar.abannews.com) that already works with affiliate programs and ranks for tool-related search queries in German. I publish honest, no-hype tool reviews and comparisons, so affiliate links are placed in genuinely useful editorial context rather than as standalone ads.

**Describe your target audience**

> German-speaking (DACH: Germany, Austria, Switzerland) solopreneurs, freelancers, small business owners and content/marketing people. They want practical tools that save time and are realistic about what AI can and can't do. Many of them create content, run websites or do their own marketing — so SEO and content optimization is a direct, recurring need for them.

**How will you promote Surfer?**

> Three channels: (1) a dedicated, honest Surfer review and "Surfer vs. alternatives" comparison on the tools directory, including who it's a good fit for and who it isn't; (2) editorial mentions in the newsletter when content/SEO is the topic; (3) integration into my SEO/content guides as the recommended tool for content optimization. All in German, all editorial, with clearly disclosed affiliate links.
