## Serie: The Seam — A Ruhr Valley Family Saga

**Serien-Beschreibung (für die KDP-Serienseite):**
```
A hundred years of coal, told through one family.

In the colliery town of Voßlohe, a miners' family is fed and owned by the pit.
When the grandfather breaks the great strike of 1905 to save his own children —
and ruins a comrade doing it — he lays a fault that is hauled up with the coal:
through two wars, dictatorship, forced labour, the boom of reconstruction, and
the slow dying of the pits, until the last shift seals the shaft in 1989.

Three generations. One inherited betrayal. The title carries three meanings:
the work shift, the seam in the rock, the seam between the classes.
```

```
It begins underground.

A miners' family arrives in the colliery town of Voßlohe, where the pit feeds
every household and owns every hour. When the great strike of 1905 tests the

breaks a comrade for it.

The first book of The Seam follows the years from that fault line through the
```
