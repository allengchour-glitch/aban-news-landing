FAQ & help

Do I need any prior knowledge to use the tool?

No. You pick your industry, give a few keywords about the topic, and hit Create. There is a step-by-step guide right on the page, made for beginners with no AI experience.

Is the tool free?

Yes, you can start for free: several texts and one roadmap per day are free. If you need more, unlock Pro — unlimited use, real AI instead of templates, export and more.

Are my inputs uploaded?

Not in the free version: texts are generated right in your browser from checked templates, nothing leaves your device. The later Pro version uses real AI; your text is then sent to an AI service — which is stated clearly.

Does the AI replace my own work?

No. The tool gives you drafts that you adapt and check. Expert advice, legal details (e.g. prices, mandatory info) and the personal contact with your customers stay with you.

What does the AI roadmap give me?

You answer three short questions and get a personal plan: where to start, which tasks pay off first, and a checklist to tick off — matched to your industry and your biggest time sink.
