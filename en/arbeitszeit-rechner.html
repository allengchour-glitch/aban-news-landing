Enter start, end and break per day — you get the net working time per day, the weekly total and, if you like, your earnings via your hourly rate. Everything local in the browser, nothing is sent.

The break is subtracted in minutes from the start–end span. If the end goes past midnight (e.g. 22:00–02:00), the next day is counted correctly. If the break is longer than the working time, the day counts as 0. Everything local, no upload.
