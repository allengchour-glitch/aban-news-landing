About Aban

There's no editorial team and no agency behind aban news. It's one person, writing every weekday morning.

Who is Aban?

My name is Allen Chour, but most people call me Aban. I'm based in Belp, Switzerland, and I write this newsletter myself – email by email, day by day.

I'm no oracle and I'm not selling you a shortcut. I read a lot about AI, sort the signal from the noise and write down what really matters for your working day. When I don't know something, I say so. When I'm wrong, I correct it.

Why aban news exists

There's a lot of talk about AI and very little of it is level-headed. Headlines promise a revolution every other day, and you end up with more questions than you started with. That's exactly where aban news comes in.

The promise is simple: Mon–Fri, five minutes, no hype. You get what has changed and what it means for you – in plain language, without buzzwords and without the pressure to buy some tool right away.

What you can expect – and what you can't

What you get

  • A short email on weekdays, read in five minutes.
  • Context in plain English, friendly and direct, without jargon.
  • Honest flags when something is still unproven or uncertain.
  • Concrete examples instead of empty promises.

What you don't get

  • No salvation promises and no get-rich-quick tricks.
  • No constant alarm and no artificial urgency.
  • No reselling of your data, no tracking.
  • No wall of jargon with nothing behind it.

What I stick to

No tracking. The website loads nothing from third parties that follows you around. What you read stays your business.

No hype. I write soberly, even when a sensational headline would get more clicks. Trust matters more to me than attention.

Direct, on equal footing. I write the way I'd explain it to a colleague – direct, friendly, without lecturing.

In short: AI is a tool, not a miracle. My job is to show you the few things that make your work easier today – and to leave out the rest that only distracts you. I'd rather send one email too few than one too many. And I treat your attention the way I'd want mine to be treated.
