What's next — public roadmap

I'm building aban news in public. Here you can see what's running right now, what's coming in 30/60/90 days and where I want to be in 12 months. If you have an opinion, reply to a newsletter email — it goes straight to me.

NOW — May 2026

What's running this week.

✅ Newsletter Mon-Fri live

Daily weekday email with 3-5 news items, one tool and one prompt. The pipeline with Make.com + Claude is in place, with proofreading by me before every send.

Status: live, stable

✅ Landing page + founding tier

Sign-up page with double opt-in, founding-member offer (EUR 69 lifetime, capped at 100), sponsoring page with a rate card.

Status: live, abannews.com

🔄 First sponsor outreach wave

Pitch emails to DACH AI-tool providers (EU-hosting focus) for pilot slots in June. Goal: 3 paid slots to validate the rate-card pricing.

Status: in progress

🔄 Newsletter swap pipeline

Three DACH solopreneur newsletters contacted for mutual recommendation. Goal: the first 100 subs without paid acquisition.

Status: in progress

NEXT 30 DAYS — June 2026

What should be done in the next month.

⏳ beehiiv migration

Switch from Gmail drafts to beehiiv (with an EU data-centre option). Better analytics, more professional sending, a DPA with the provider. Current subscribers are migrated automatically.

Trigger: at 100 active subs

⏳ Make pipeline v2

The current pipeline runs, but it's too fragile. v2 with error handlers, fallback routes, an RSS cache and a Notion DB for topic tracking. Goal: one day off possible without the pipeline breaking.

Effort: ~8h setup

⏳ First 3 sponsor deals

Close three sponsor slots from the pilot wave (EUR 250 each). Validates the rate card and gives substance for the “advertise with us" page.

Goal: EUR 750 sponsor revenue in June

⏳ 500-sub target

Growth via newsletter swaps, LinkedIn posts, Reddit comments (helping-first strategy). 500 subs is the threshold at which the sponsor rate card can rise to EUR 350.

Goal: 500 verified subs by the end of June

60-90 DAYS — July/August 2026

What becomes visible in the quarter after.

⏳ English sister eu.abannews.com

English-language version for AI pros in the EU outside DACH (Netherlands, Nordics, Southern EU). Same tone, same anti-hype style, but in EN. Shared pipeline, separate newsletter.

Trigger: at 1000 DE subs

⏳ Podcast pilot

A ten-episode pilot “Anti-Hype Talk" — Aban interviews DACH solopreneurs who actually use AI tools, without hype vocabulary. Format: 30-45 min, monthly.

Hosting: still open — to be decided once the pilot starts.

⏳ Founding-member cap at 100

Once 100 founding slots are filled: close the offer, and Premium switches to a regular monthly subscription (~EUR 9/month). Founding members keep theirs for life.

Goal: 100 founding slots by Q3

⏳ B2B team tier

For DACH SMEs: a team subscription with central billing, a DPA, and an “internal brief" format (a 10-min weekly summary for marketing teams). Pricing: EUR 39/month per team of up to 10 people.

Goal: the first 5 team customers

VISION — 12 months

Where I want to be in a year. Ambitious, but not delusional.

🎯 10,000 subscribers

Grow from the first 500 pilot subs to 10k active subscribers. Realistic with a consistent Mon-Fri cadence and 3-5 swap pipelines per month. No growth hack — substance and consistency.

🎯 EUR 15,000 MRR

A sponsoring mix (5-7 slots/month at ~EUR 600), premium subs (~200 paying members at EUR 9), a B2B team tier (~15 teams at EUR 39). Solo-sustainable without an investor.

🎯 Own conference “Anti-Hype Summit DACH"

A one-day conference in Zurich or Vienna for 200-300 attendees. Speakers: DACH solopreneurs and operators who use AI without hype. Ticket price around EUR 199, with a newsletter-member discount.

🎯 Book

“Anti-Hype — How German solopreneurs use AI without the bullshit." Self-published, written from the 200+ newsletter issues. Probably on Amazon KDP plus an eBook edition.

Already here: a free short version is already available as a free eBook — the full version comes later.
