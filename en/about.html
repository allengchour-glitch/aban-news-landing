I'm Aban. I write aban news.

One person. One newsletter. Mon–Fri morning, in German, for people who use AI daily and don't have patience for hype-driven weekly digests.

Why aban news exists

I'm Allen Chour — friends call me Aban. Solopreneur, based in Switzerland. I've been using AI tools daily for three years for writing, research and automation. What got on my nerves: scrolling through twelve newsletters, LinkedIn posts and Reddit threads every morning to find the few updates that actually mattered.

Most German-language AI newsletters are weekly (too slow) or full of marketing speak (too hype). English daily newsletters like TLDR AI exist — but there's translation friction plus a US-tool bias.

So I'm building aban news. Daily, in German, hand-curated. Like a smart friend over coffee — telling you what's going on, and also telling you when a tool isn't worth your time.

What I do, what I don't

I do

  • Write every issue myself, in German
  • Test tools before I recommend them (minimum 30 min on a real task)
  • Link every source — claims you can check
  • Read and reply to every email personally
  • Push back when something is hype, even from sponsors

I don't

  • Use ghostwriters or an agency
  • Copy-paste press releases
  • Run tracking pixels or sell data
  • Translate this newsletter into English (yet)
  • Promise "10x your productivity"

The setup, briefly

The pipeline runs nightly: Make.com pulls RSS from five German AI sites, Anthropic Claude (Sonnet 4.7) drafts a version, a forbidden-phrases regex catches hype words, I review and rewrite, the mail ships at 06:30. Half automated, fully hand-edited.

Tech stack on the site: static HTML on GitHub Pages, no WordPress, no PHP, no cookie banner needed. Mail: Gmail drafts in the pilot phase, migrating to beehiiv with EU datacenter option once subscriber count justifies it.

For the curious: I picked Claude because it writes German better than the alternatives I tested and follows anti-hype constraints more reliably. Re-evaluated quarterly.

Background

Before aban news I was running a Shopify store on the side and building newsletter-automation setups for other operators. Three years of daily Claude/ChatGPT use, before that ten years of marketing and content work for SMBs.

German-speaking, English-fluent, French-rusty. Based in Belp, Switzerland — a small commune outside Bern. The newsletter is written here and read across Germany, Austria and Switzerland.

If you want to reach me directly: hallo@abannews.com. Real inbox, I read everything.
