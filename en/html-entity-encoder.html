Encode HTML entities

Convert special characters like &, <, > into HTML entities — or back. Handy for showing code in HTML or resolving entities. Optionally also Unicode as numeric entities. Everything local in your browser, nothing is sent.

Result

When encoding, & < > " ' are always converted; with the option, additionally all characters above ASCII as numeric entities (e.g. &#8364; for €). When decoding, named entities (e.g. &auml;) as well as decimal (&#228;) and hexadecimal (&#xE4;) are recognised. Everything local, no upload.
