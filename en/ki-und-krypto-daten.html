①The AI-crypto sector: what the terms mean

When "AI" and "crypto" are mentioned together, very different things are usually meant. The table separates them — and says honestly what has substance and what is mostly narrative.

AI-crypto categories — where they stand. As of June 2026.
CategoryWhat it isMaturity
Decentralised computeMarketplaces that rent out unused GPUs for AI training (instead of big cloud providers).early
AI agents with a walletPrograms that trigger transactions on their own. Technically exciting, barely tested in practice.experimental
Data & model marketplacesTrading training data or model access via tokens.early
"AI coins" / meme tokensTokens with "AI" in the name, often with no working product behind them.mostly hype
AI in analysisAI evaluates on-chain data (fraud detection, compliance). That genuinely works.established

Maturity is rated by publicly documented product use, not market capitalisation. "Hype" means: lots of attention, little demonstrable value.

③AI in crypto trading: what it can and can't do

This is the area with the loudest promises — "AI bot doubles your account". Soberly viewed, AI can do quite a lot in trading, but the promised thing least of all.

A realistic view of AI in crypto trading.
Use caseWorks?Note
Predict prices reliablynoMarginally above chance short-term, usually gone after fees.
Spot patterns & anomaliesyesOn-chain oddities, wash trading, fraud.
Evaluate news/sentimentpartlyHelps with the overview, but easy to manipulate.
Execute rules with disciplineyesBots stick to a stop-loss — humans don't.
"Guaranteed profits"noAnyone promising this wants your money, not your success.

The honest rule of thumb: in trading, AI is a discipline and analysis tool, not a crystal ball. The biggest measurable advantage is often that a bot doesn't trade out of fear or greed — not that it knows the future.

Frequently asked questions

Can AI predict crypto prices?
Not reliably. AI spots patterns in historical data, but crypto markets hang on news, regulation and sentiment — things no model can predict with certainty. In the short term there are studies with slightly better-than-chance hits, but after fees and in real market phases the edge usually vanishes. Anyone selling you an AI that knows the price is selling a promise.
Is an AI trading bot worth it?
As a discipline tool for a strategy you understand yourself — maybe. As a "money-printing machine" — no. Most bots sold freely earn from selling the bot, not from trading. If a bot reliably made profits, nobody would sell it for €49 a month.
Is crypto "bad for the climate" because of its electricity use?
It depends on the mechanism. Proof-of-work (Bitcoin) genuinely uses a lot of electricity. Proof-of-stake (Ethereum since 2022) cut consumption by more than 99 %. So the blanket statement "crypto eats electricity" is only still true for part of the market.
Do I have to pay tax on crypto gains?
In Germany often yes (except after more than a year held), in Austria a flat 27.5 %, in Switzerland usually not for private gains — but with wealth tax instead. These are rough key figures, not advice. Document your trades and, when in doubt, ask a tax professional.
