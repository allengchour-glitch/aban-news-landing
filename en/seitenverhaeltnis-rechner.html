Calculate aspect ratio

Compute the missing width or height for an aspect ratio like 16:9 — or reduce two dimensions to their lowest ratio. Handy for images, videos, thumbnails and print. Everything runs locally in your browser, nothing is sent.

Aspect ratio
:

"Calculate dimension": enter the ratio and ONE dimension — the other is filled in (the last edited field stays). "Reduce ratio": two dimensions are reduced via the greatest common divisor (e.g. 1920×1080 → 16:9). All local, no upload.
