Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the estimate?

It's an estimate — rule of thumb ~4 characters/token (English), German a bit more. Every model counts a little differently. For the exact number there are official tokenizers. For budgeting it's enough.

Why do I enter the price myself?

Because model prices change and differ by provider/currency. With your price, the calculation is always right.

Are my texts sent anywhere?

No, everything runs locally in your browser. No upload, no account, no tracking.
