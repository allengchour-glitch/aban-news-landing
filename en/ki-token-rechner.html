Common questions

How accurate is the estimate?

An estimate — rule of thumb ~4 chars/token (English), a bit more otherwise. Models differ slightly. For exact numbers there are official tokenizers. For budgeting it's enough.

Why enter the price myself?

Because model prices change and differ by provider/currency. With your price the maths is always right.

Is my text sent anywhere?

No, everything runs locally in the browser. No upload, no account, no tracking.
