Margin & markup

Fair prices instead of gut feeling: enter your cost and the selling price — or work out the required price from a desired margin or markup. You see profit, margin % and markup % at a glance. Everything local in the browser, nothing is sent.

CHF
CHF

Margin (return on sales) = profit ÷ selling price. Markup (cost surcharge) = profit ÷ cost. Example: cost 50, price 80 → profit 30, margin 37.5 %, markup 60 %. A margin of 100 % is impossible (the price would then be infinite). Everything local, no upload.
