Generate a Border-Radius

Set rounded corners with sliders — all together or individually, in px or percent — see them live and copy the finished border-radius code. Everything local in your browser, nothing is sent.

CSS 

    
With "Link all corners", the first slider controls all four. In percent (up to 50 %) you get elliptical corners — 50 % on all corners makes an oval or a circle. The code is collapsed into a short border-radius when possible. Everything local, no upload.
