The Valley Holds Its Breath

A generational drama in three volumes

Across three generations, a family in a remote Swiss mountain valley keeps a guilt under lock: a death recorded as an accident, a letter never sent, and the silent agreement of an entire village not to ask. Written chapter by chapter with Claude Opus — now complete: three volumes, 36 chapters, free as ePub.

The three volumes

Volume 1 The Slope Riedmatt, 1961–1963

Marlene marries in from outside onto a farm on the slope and learns quickly that here you heat the house with silence. As the dam is built, something shifts for everyone — and a single morning decides what will go unsaid for decades.

The generation that founds the guilt, and the woman who is first to carry it.

Volume 2 The Middle Riedmatt, 1991–1992

Thirty years later Theo takes over the indebted inn while the valley dreams of ski tourism. In a box in the attic he finds papers that do not add up — and faces the question of whether to open the truth or use it.

The generation that senses what the prosperity rests on, and fails to find the word.

Volume 3 The Water Riedmatt, present day

In a drought summer Eva returns as a hydrologist to inspect the ageing dam. The falling water level exposes what has lain submerged since 1962 — and forces the third generation into the decision the first one faced: speak, or carry it on.

The generation that has to look, because the water gives back what was given to it.

How it was made

This trilogy is a writing experiment: a plot bible fixes the world, the characters, the tone, and the arc across all three volumes; from it, Claude Opus writes the chapters one by one, true to continuity, volume by volume. The English edition is a faithful, sentence-by-sentence translation of the German original. The code behind it is open in the project (ki-schriftsteller/).
