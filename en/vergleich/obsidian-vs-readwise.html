Obsidian vs Readwise Reader: what matters when choosing

When you are facing the choice between Obsidian and Readwise Reader, at its core it comes down to how you collect, process and retrieve information. Obsidian is like your personal digital notebook, in which you can link your thoughts freely. It is ideal if you want to build a system that adapts to your needs, you value maximum control over your data, and you are willing to invest a little time in getting up to speed in order to create your very own knowledge base.

Readwise Reader, on the other hand, is more of a smart reading assistant that helps you read articles, emails and PDFs efficiently, highlight important passages and easily find those highlights again later. It is the better choice if your main focus is on consuming and annotating external content, you prefer ease of use, and you are willing to pay for a service that takes a lot of work off your hands. So think carefully: do you want to build a flexible system yourself, or would you rather use a specialised service that makes reading and managing content easier for you?

FAQ

I just want to read and highlight my web articles and PDFs. Which tool is better for me?

For purely reading and highlighting web articles and PDFs, Readwise Reader is the clearly better choice. It is built specifically to import and read content and to manage your highlights centrally, without you having to worry about the organisation.

I want to take my own notes and connect them together to develop new ideas. What fits better?

Obsidian is ideal for that. It is a note-taking tool that lets you link your thoughts and notes freely, much like a brain. You can build a personal knowledge network that helps you spot connections and develop new ideas.

Is one of the tools free to use?

Obsidian is free to use in its basic version for personal use. Readwise Reader, on the other hand, is a paid service that requires a subscription to use all features.

I'm concerned about data protection. Which tool stores my data more securely?

With Obsidian your data sits locally on your device, so you have full control over it. Readwise Reader stores your data in the cloud, which means you have to trust the provider. For maximum data sovereignty, Obsidian is the safer choice.
