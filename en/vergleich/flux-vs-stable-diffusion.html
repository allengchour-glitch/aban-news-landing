Flux vs Stable Diffusion: what matters when choosing

You're facing the choice between Flux and Stable Diffusion for creating images with artificial intelligence. To make the right decision, first consider exactly what you have in mind. If you want to get good results quickly and easily without diving deep into the technical side, and ease of use matters to you, then Flux might be the better choice for you. It's often more user-friendly and takes many technical details off your hands, which makes getting started easier and makes it ideal for occasional use or for users without prior knowledge.

If, on the other hand, you want maximum control over the entire image-generation process, value the ability to adapt your own models or achieve special effects, and are willing to engage more deeply with the subject, then Stable Diffusion is probably the more fitting solution. It gives you more freedom and customisation options, but it also requires a certain technical understanding and the willingness to invest time in learning and experimenting. And if data protection and the ability to run the software locally on your own computer matter to you, Stable Diffusion is often at an advantage.

FAQ

I'm a complete beginner. Which tool is easier to learn?

For absolute beginners, Flux is usually easier to learn. It's often designed so you get good-looking results with just a few clicks, without having to understand many settings. Stable Diffusion requires a bit more learning, but in return offers more possibilities.

I want to create images for my small business. Which is better for professional use?

For professional use, especially if you need specific styles or high customisability, Stable Diffusion often offers more flexibility and control. Flux can be good for quick drafts or less demanding tasks, but Stable Diffusion lets you achieve more precise and unique results that you can adapt to your brand identity.

Can I use the images I create commercially?

Yes, in principle you can use images created with both tools commercially. However, always pay attention to the exact licensing terms of the particular version or platform you're using, as there can be differences here. Inform yourself about the usage rights beforehand to be on the safe side.

Do I have to pay for these tools?

Stable Diffusion is open-source in its basic form and can often be used for free if you install it on your own computer. For Flux or certain online versions of Stable Diffusion there may be costs, for example for compute power or advanced features. But there are also free trial periods or limited free versions.
