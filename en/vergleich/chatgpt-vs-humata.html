ChatGPT vs Humata: what matters when choosing

Facing the choice between ChatGPT and Humata and wondering which tool suits you better? The decision depends heavily on what exactly you have in mind and how deep you want to dive into the subject. If you are looking for a versatile conversation partner that helps you with texts, ideas or general questions, ChatGPT is often the first choice. It is like a Swiss army knife for text that stands by you quickly and easily, without you having to spend a long time learning it.

Humata, on the other hand, plays to its strengths when it comes to analysing and understanding large amounts of your own documents. Imagine you have a stack of important papers and want to quickly find the core information or connections — Humata is ideal for that. It specialises in searching and answering questions based on your uploaded PDFs or other documents, which makes it particularly useful for research, legal work or extensive project work, where data protection and control over your own data play a big role.

FAQ

I have lots of my own documents that I want to understand and summarise. Which tool is better?

For analysing your own documents, such as PDFs or reports, Humata is the clearly better choice. It is built specifically to search through your uploaded content and give you precise answers based on it.

I need a tool to help me write emails, brainstorm or create text. Which one do I pick?

If you need support with writing, finding ideas or general text tasks, ChatGPT is the better option. It is a versatile text generator that can help you with the most varied phrasings and content.

Is one of the tools easier to use if I have no prior knowledge?

ChatGPT is generally easier and more intuitive for beginners. You simply enter your question or instruction and get an answer. Humata requires a basic understanding of uploading and managing documents, but it is also designed to be user-friendly.

Does data protection matter if I process sensitive information?

Yes, data protection is an important point. If you want to process sensitive or business-critical documents, you should look closely at the providers' data protection terms. Humata often advertises a stronger focus on data security and control over your own data, since the processing is often based on your uploaded documents.
