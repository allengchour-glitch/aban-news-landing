Cap.so vs Loom: what matters when choosing

If you want to record and share short video messages quickly and easily without spending much time on editing, Loom is often the simpler choice. It's known for its easy operation and fast sharing, ideal for internal communication or short explainer videos. The learning curve is flat, so you can get started right away, even if you've never used such a tool before.

Cap.so, on the other hand, is the better option if you value more control over your videos, for example through customisable templates or advanced editing features that help you create more professional content. It's well suited if you often have recurring video formats or want to align your videos more closely with your brand image. Also, if data privacy in the DACH region is especially important to you, Cap.so could be the more fitting choice thanks to its focus on European standards.

FAQ

What is the main difference if I just want to quickly record a screen video?

Loom is often faster and more direct here. You start the recording with a few clicks and can share the link straight away. Cap.so also offers quick recordings, but often has more options in the background that you don't always need right away.

I make a lot of explainer videos for clients. Which tool helps me look more professional?

Cap.so offers more here through templates and advanced editing to make your videos look more consistent and professional. For example, you can add intros or outros more easily that match your brand image.

Is one of the tools better if I'm concerned about data privacy?

Cap.so places a stronger focus on European data privacy standards and server locations in the DACH region, which is an important point for many companies here. Loom is a US company whose data is often also processed in the US.

I'm not very tech-savvy. Which tool is easier to learn?

Loom is considered extremely user-friendly and has a very low learning curve. You can usually get started straight away without a long onboarding. Cap.so is also easy, but offers more features that might require some initial familiarisation.
