Reflect vs Tana: what matters when choosing

When you face the choice between Reflect and Tana, it essentially comes down to how you most like to organise and process information. Reflect is ideal if you are looking for a simple, fast and straightforward note app that focuses on the essentials: capturing your thoughts and finding them again easily. It is perfect for a quick start, when you have no appetite for a long onboarding and prefer a clear structure organised around a calendar.

Tana, on the other hand, is the right choice if you want to build a powerful system that goes well beyond simple notes. It is meant for those who are willing to invest some time in learning a flexible tool in order to manage complex projects, build knowledge bases or even map out entire workflows. If the ability to link data together and create your own structures matters to you, Tana is the stronger option, even if the learning curve is steeper.

FAQ

I'm not a tech expert. Which tool is easier to learn?

Reflect is significantly easier to learn. You can start straight away, make notes and find them again via the calendar. Tana takes more getting used to, because it offers a great many features and customisation options that you first have to understand.

I want to store my notes securely and not have my data end up everywhere. Which is better?

Both tools value data protection, but there are differences. Reflect stores your notes locally on your device and syncs them encrypted, which offers a high degree of control. Tana is also secure, but its more complex structure and cloud connection bring other aspects you should weigh up depending on your individual preferences.

I need a tool I can adapt to my very specific workflows. Which is more flexible?

Tana clearly has the edge here. It is designed for you to adapt it to your own needs, create your own templates and link information together. Reflect is optimised more for a predefined, linear way of working and offers fewer customisation options.

I want to find my notes quickly without having to search for long. Which tool helps me with that?

Reflect is very good at finding notes again quickly via the date or a simple search. Tana also offers a strong search function, but its strength lies more in linking information, so you find connections and not just individual notes.
