Cerebras Inference vs Groq: what matters when choosing

Facing the choice between Cerebras Inference and Groq and wondering which tool fits you better? Quite simply: if you're looking for a highly specialised, tailored solution for very large and complex AI models that you've trained yourself and that need maximum performance in use, then Cerebras Inference is often the better choice. It's aimed at companies and research institutions with deep technical understanding and the need to use their own, often unique AI models efficiently.

Groq, on the other hand, is ideal if you value extremely fast responses from existing, common language models and want easy integration into your existing applications. It's more accessible for developers and companies who want quick and uncomplicated access to powerful AI models without having to worry about the underlying hardware. Remember: it's not about which is better, but which fits your specific use case and resources better.

FAQ

What is the main difference between Cerebras Inference and Groq for me as a user?

Cerebras Inference is more geared towards running your own, often very large and specialised AI models that you have developed yourself. Groq focuses on making existing, common language models extremely fast, so you can get started right away without building your own models.

I have no idea about AI models. Which one is easier to use?

Groq is much easier for you. There you use ready-made AI models, similar to a template into which you just insert your text. With Cerebras Inference you would need to bring your own model and know how to optimise it.

Does data privacy matter if I process sensitive data?

Yes, absolutely. With Cerebras Inference you often have more control over your data, as you can run your models on your own infrastructure or in dedicated environments. With Groq you use a service whose data privacy terms you should check, since your requests run through their servers.

What if I only want to use AI occasionally and don't have a big budget?

For occasional use and smaller budgets, Groq is probably the more fitting choice. It often offers a usage-based pricing model, whereas Cerebras Inference is more geared towards larger investments in specialised hardware or services.
