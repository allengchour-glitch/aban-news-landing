Arc Search vs Brave Search: what matters when choosing

When you're facing the choice between Arc Search and Brave Search, a lot depends on what matters most to you when browsing. If you're looking for a completely new way of finding information that presents results to you pre-sorted and summarised, then Arc Search, which works more like a smart assistant, might be more interesting for you. It's ideal if you're willing to embrace a new workflow and appreciate the convenience of pre-digested information processing, even if it means using a new browser.

Brave Search, on the other hand, is the better choice if data protection and privacy come first for you and you prefer a traditional search engine that doesn't build personal profiles of you. It's for you if you want a familiar search experience that fits seamlessly into the privacy-friendly Brave browser and gives you full control over your data. So if you value a clear, unfiltered search and have no desire to experiment with new interfaces, Brave Search is the more reliable option.

FAQ

What is the biggest difference between Arc Search and Brave Search?

Arc Search tries to do the work for you by summarising search results and presenting them as a kind of web page. Brave Search gives you classic search results, but focuses on your privacy and blocks ads and trackers by default.

Is one of the tools better suited to people who aren't tech-savvy?

Brave Search is easier to understand because it behaves like any other search engine, just more privacy-friendly. Arc Search takes some getting used to, as it offers a whole new way of presenting information that you first have to learn.

Which tool is better if data protection is very important to me?

Brave Search has a clear advantage here. It is designed for data protection from the ground up, collects no personal data and offers you an anonymous search experience. Arc Search is primarily a browser with an integrated search function, whose main focus is not exclusively on the data protection of the search function.

Do I have to pay for either of the tools?

No, both search engines are free to use in their basic function. Arc Search is part of the free Arc browser, and Brave Search is also free, integrated into the Brave browser or usable as a standalone search engine.
