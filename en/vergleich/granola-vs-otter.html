Granola vs Otter.ai: what matters when choosing

When you are facing the choice between Granola and Otter.ai, a lot depends on what matters more to you and how you work. Granola is often the better choice if you value data protection and would rather not have your data processed in the US, since it is a European solution. It scores points if you are looking for a simple, uncomplicated solution that integrates quickly into your everyday work and that has no long learning curve.

Otter.ai, on the other hand, is ideal if you are looking for an established tool with many features that already works well with other applications. It is especially strong if you frequently have English-language meetings and need advanced features such as automatic summaries or the identification of different speakers. Bear in mind here that your data could be processed outside the DACH region, which depending on your requirements can be a deciding factor.

FAQ

Which tool is easier to use if I have never worked with AI tools before?

Granola is often a little more intuitive and more simply structured, which makes getting started easier for newcomers. Otter.ai does offer more features, but it can feel a bit overwhelming at first if you have no experience with such tools yet.

I have a lot of meetings in German. Which tool is better here?

As a European development, Granola is often better tuned to the German-speaking region and delivers more precise results here. Otter.ai is primarily optimised for English, but it can also handle German, although the accuracy can vary.

Is one of the tools better if I want to share my meeting notes with colleagues later?

Both tools offer features for sharing transcripts. Otter.ai often has somewhat more mature collaboration features here, for editing notes together and adding comments. Granola focuses more on pure transcription and simple sharing.

What if I only need to transcribe a meeting occasionally?

For occasional use, both tools often offer free basic versions or flexible subscription models. Granola could score points here with its simplicity and focus on the DACH region, while Otter.ai with its broader feature range can also be attractive for the occasional user if they want to use more features later.
