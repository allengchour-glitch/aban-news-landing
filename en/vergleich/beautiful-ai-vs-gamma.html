Beautiful.ai vs Gamma: what matters when choosing

If you want to create attractive presentations quickly and easily, without spending a long time on design questions, then Beautiful.ai is probably the better choice for you. It's like a smart assistant that takes many design decisions off your hands and ensures your result always looks professional. That's especially handy if you're short on time or don't see yourself as a design expert and simply want to get your message across clearly.

Gamma, on the other hand, is ideal if you need more flexibility with the layout and also want to design other formats such as documents or websites. It gives you more freedom to arrange content exactly as you imagine it, while still being very user-friendly. So if the ability to occasionally break away from the given structures and present your content in varied ways matters to you, then you should take a closer look at Gamma.

FAQ

What's the biggest difference between Beautiful.ai and Gamma?

Beautiful.ai focuses strongly on automated design for presentations, whereas Gamma gives you more freedom in designing presentations, documents and websites, without getting complicated in the process.

Which tool is better if I have no design skills?

Beautiful.ai has the edge here, as it takes many design decisions off your hands and ensures your presentation always looks professional, even if you have no idea about design.

Can I create content other than just presentations with both tools?

With Beautiful.ai you focus primarily on presentations. Gamma, on the other hand, is more versatile and also lets you create attractive documents and simple websites.

Which tool is better suited if I want to share my presentations with others?

Both tools offer good sharing options. Beautiful.ai specialises in presentations, while Gamma can also create interactive documents and websites that you can simply share via a link.
