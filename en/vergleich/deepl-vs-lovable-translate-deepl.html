DeepL vs Lingo.dev: what matters when choosing

When you are choosing between DeepL and Lingo.dev, a lot depends on exactly what you want to translate and how you work. DeepL is ideal when you need to translate whole texts, documents or websites quickly and precisely while valuing high language quality that often sounds very natural. It is a good choice for anyone looking for a simple solution for direct use, without having to worry about complicated settings, and also for companies that need a reliable translation API (an interface for programs).

Lingo.dev, on the other hand, is more for you if you often need to translate recurring text snippets or short sentences while ensuring consistent terminology (always the same terms) across different projects. It is especially useful for teams that work on texts together and want to maintain a kind of glossary (dictionary) or style guidelines for their translations. If data protection — especially in the DACH region — is important to you and you are looking for a solution that gives you more control over your translation data, Lingo.dev could also be the more suitable choice, as it is often more strongly tailored to these needs.

FAQ

Which tool is better for translating long documents?

For long documents, DeepL is usually the better choice. It is built to translate whole texts and files efficiently and at high quality, whereas Lingo.dev is more optimised for smaller text snippets or sentence fragments.

I need translations for my website. Which tool helps me most there?

If you want to translate your entire website, DeepL often offers more direct integrations or APIs that make this process easier. Lingo.dev can be useful for translating recurring sentences or menu items consistently, but for the full scope of a website DeepL is usually more practical.

Which of the two tools is easier to use if I have no prior knowledge?

DeepL is generally more intuitive and easier to use, especially when you just want to quickly translate a text. You enter your text and get a translation right away. Lingo.dev may require a little more familiarisation to make the most of its features for consistent translations.

Is one of the tools better for data protection, especially in Germany, Austria or Switzerland?

Lingo.dev often places a stronger focus on data protection and compliance with European standards, which can be relevant for users in the DACH region. DeepL also offers data protection options, but with Lingo.dev this aspect is often a central selling point, especially for companies.
