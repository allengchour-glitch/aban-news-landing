ChatGPT vs Perplexity: what matters when choosing

Trying to decide between ChatGPT and Perplexity and wondering which tool suits you better? It's simple: if you're looking for a versatile tool for creative text, brainstorming or rewriting content, ChatGPT is often the better choice. It's like a Swiss army knife for language and helps you develop ideas or phrase text without having to worry about sources. And if you're just getting started with AI and want an easily accessible tool, ChatGPT's simple operation often makes it the ideal entry point.

If, on the other hand, you're looking for a tool that gives you precise answers to factual questions and shows exactly where that information comes from, then Perplexity is your candidate. It's like a smart search engine that not only gives you the answer but also supplies the sources to back it up – ideal for research, school papers or when you simply want to know whether a piece of information is true. And if data protection matters a lot to you or you value integration into other services, there are subtle differences here worth keeping in mind.

FAQ

I need the sources behind the information. Which tool is better?

Clearly Perplexity. It is designed not only to give you answers but also to cite the exact sources the information comes from. ChatGPT usually does not name sources, as it is geared more towards generating text.

I want to write text, develop ideas or rewrite content. Which tool is better?

For creative writing, brainstorming or rewriting text, ChatGPT is the better choice. It is trained to generate fluent and varied text and can help you with a wide range of language tasks.

Which tool is easier to use if I have no experience with AI yet?

ChatGPT is often considered easier and more intuitive for beginners. The interface is very clear and the way you interact with it is straightforward, which makes getting started easier.

Are there differences in cost or the availability of a free version?

Both tools offer free basic versions that are sufficient for many use cases. For advanced features or more intensive use, both have paid subscriptions. The exact prices and feature sets can change, so you should check the current offers directly on the respective websites.
