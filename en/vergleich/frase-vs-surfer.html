Frase vs Surfer SEO: what matters when choosing

If you're facing the choice between Frase and Surfer SEO, a lot depends on what you really need. Frase is often the better choice if you write a lot of your own text content and are looking for support with research and outlining. It helps you quickly see which topics you should cover and gives you a kind of writing assistant that makes suggestions as you write. And if your budget is a bit smaller and you prefer a simpler interface, Frase could be the right partner for you.

Surfer SEO, on the other hand, shines when you already have finished texts or commission external authors and want to put them through their paces. It is very strong at optimising your texts precisely for the requirements of search engines and telling you exactly which words or phrases are still missing. So if you're looking for detailed analysis and concrete optimisation suggestions for search engine placement, and you're willing to invest a bit more time getting up to speed, then Surfer SEO is probably the more suitable solution for you.

FAQ

I'm a solo copywriter and write all my blog articles myself. Which tool fits me better?

As a solo copywriter who writes a lot themselves, you'll probably get more out of Frase. It supports you directly during the writing process by helping you research and structure content as you write. That saves you time on preparation and gives you direct feedback.

I have a team of copywriters and want to make sure our content ranks well on Google. What's the better choice?

For a team aiming at search engine rankings, Surfer SEO is often the better choice. It offers in-depth analysis and concrete optimisation suggestions that your team can use to optimise existing or new content precisely for Google. It's a strong tool for fine-tuning.

I'm not very familiar with AI tools yet. Which tool is easier to learn?

Frase is generally considered a bit simpler and more intuitive to use, especially for beginners. The interface is clearer and the features are often more directly geared towards the writing process, which makes getting started easier. Surfer SEO has more features and needs a little more time to get to grips with.

Does data protection matter when I decide on one of the tools?

Both tools are US providers, which means your data could be processed in the US. If data protection to German or European standards (DACH) is especially important to you, you should look closely at the respective privacy policies before use and check whether they meet your requirements.
