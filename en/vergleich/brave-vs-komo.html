Brave Search vs Komo Search: what matters when choosing

If you're looking for a search engine that takes your privacy seriously while also offering a good mix of traditional search results and AI-powered summaries, then Brave Search is a good choice for you. It's especially useful if you value a transparent way of working and don't want to be influenced by personalised search results. The learning curve is flat, as the operation resembles that of a classic search engine, supplemented by handy AI features you can use when needed.

Komo Search, on the other hand, is ideal if you want a search engine built from the ground up for quickly finding answers via AI and often delivering a concise summary directly. It works well if you frequently have specific questions and don't want to wade through long lists of links first. Bear in mind that Komo Search is still relatively new and constantly evolving, which means features can change and integration with other services is not yet as comprehensive as with more established providers. The operation is intuitive, as it focuses heavily on directly answering your questions.

FAQ

Which search engine is better for protecting my data?

Brave Search places a strong focus on data protection. It does not track your search queries and does not build a profile of you. Komo Search is also privacy-friendly, as it does not create personalised profiles, but Brave has a longer and more transparent history here.

Which search engine delivers faster answers to complex questions?

Komo Search is designed to give you concise answers quickly via AI summaries, which is often faster for complex questions. Brave Search also offers AI summaries, but the focus is more on providing links to sources.

Are there any hidden costs with either search engine?

No, both search engines are free to use in their basic function. There are no hidden costs or subscriptions for using the search features. Any premium features are clearly marked.

Can I find 'normal' websites with both search engines if I don't want an AI answer?

Yes, absolutely. Alongside the AI-generated answers, both search engines also show you traditional search results in the form of links to websites. You always have the choice of whether to use the AI summary or browse the original sources yourself.
