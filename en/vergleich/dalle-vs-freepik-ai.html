DALL-E 3 vs Freepik AI Suite: what matters when choosing

If you're facing the choice between DALL-E 3 and the Freepik AI Suite, a lot depends on exactly what you have in mind and how much experience you bring. If you're looking for an easy way to create unique images from text without much editing, and you're happy to slot into an existing system like ChatGPT or Bing Image Creator, then DALL-E 3 is often the more direct choice. It shines when it comes to turning creative ideas into images quickly, without having to worry about details like commercial-use licences, as long as you observe the terms of the platform that has DALL-E 3 built in.

The Freepik AI Suite, on the other hand, is the better option if you want more control over the whole design process and often work with templates or existing graphics. It lets you not only generate images, but also edit them directly, combine them with other design elements and adapt them for specific projects. So if you regularly create graphics for social media, presentations or websites and value an integrated solution that also gives you access to a large library of stock photos and vectors, then Freepik is the more flexible and comprehensive choice for you.

FAQ

I'm a complete beginner. Which tool is easier to learn?

DALL-E 3 is often easier for beginners, since you only have to enter a text description to get an image. The Freepik AI Suite offers more possibilities, but it also requires a basic understanding of image editing and design concepts.

I need images for my social media channels. Which tool is better suited?

For social media, DALL-E 3 can quickly deliver unique images that grab attention. But the Freepik AI Suite is better if you want to drop images into existing templates or combine them with text and other graphics, since it offers more editing options.

Can I use the generated images commercially?

With DALL-E 3, commercial use depends on the terms of the platform you use it through (e.g. ChatGPT Plus or Bing Image Creator). With the Freepik AI Suite, the licences for the generated content are usually more clearly defined and often intended for commercial purposes, especially if you have a paid subscription.

I don't just want to generate images, I also want to edit them. Which tool is better?

Clearly the Freepik AI Suite. It is designed to let you not only generate but also directly edit and integrate images into other designs. DALL-E 3 focuses primarily on pure image generation.
