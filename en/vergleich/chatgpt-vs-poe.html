FAQ

What's the biggest difference between ChatGPT and Poe for me as a beginner?

ChatGPT is OpenAI's AI directly — essentially the original. Poe is a platform that gives you access to various AIs, including ChatGPT, but also other models you can try out.

Which tool is easier to use if I've never used an AI before?

ChatGPT is often very direct and intuitive, as it focuses on communicating with a single model. Because of the choice between different bots, Poe can require a bit more getting used to at first, but in return it offers more possibilities.

Do I have to expect hidden costs with either tool?

Both tools offer free basic versions that are often enough to get started. For advanced features or faster responses, both have paid subscriptions whose scope and pricing models can differ.

Can I save my own templates or instructions to reuse them again and again?

Yes, with both tools you can review your requests and the AI's responses in the chat history. Poe additionally lets you create your own bots that you can equip with specific instructions, which comes very close to a saved template.
