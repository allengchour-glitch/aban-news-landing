Apify vs Browse AI: what matters when choosing

If you want to collect data from websites, you often face the choice between tools like Apify and Browse AI. Your decision depends heavily on how technically savvy you are and how complex your requirements are. If you need a simple solution that you can set up quickly without writing much code, and you mainly want to grab specific information from a few pages, then Browse AI could be the better choice for you.

If, on the other hand, you are looking for a flexible platform that can also handle very complex tasks — for example collecting data across many pages with special rules — and you are not afraid of a bit more technical effort, then Apify is probably the more suitable solution. It offers you more options for customisation and automation, but it also requires you to dig deeper into the subject. So think carefully about how much time and technical knowledge you want to invest and how extensive your data projects are.

FAQ

What is the main difference between Apify and Browse AI for someone with no prior knowledge?

Browse AI is like a simple assistant that helps you copy specific information from websites without having to code. Apify is more like a workshop where you can build very custom tools to collect data — that requires a bit more learning, but it also offers far more possibilities.

When is Browse AI the better choice for me?

Browse AI is ideal when you need data from websites quickly and without fuss, for example prices from product pages or contact details from directories. It is especially good for users who have no programming skills and prefer a simple interface to get their tasks done.

When should I consider Apify?

Apify is the right choice when your projects are more complex, for example when you need to collect large amounts of data from many different pages or apply special logic during collection. It is aimed at users who are willing to invest a little more time in learning the ropes in order to make use of the full flexibility and customisability.

How important is data protection when choosing between these tools for the DACH region?

Data protection always matters. Both tools process data that you give them or that they collect. It is crucial that you check the respective privacy policies carefully and understand where the data is stored and how it is processed, in order to meet the requirements in the DACH region.
