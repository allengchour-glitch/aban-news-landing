Claude vs Perplexity: what matters when choosing

Facing the choice between Claude and Perplexity and wondering which tool suits you better? It's simple: if you are looking for an AI that helps you write, summarises texts or develops ideas, while valuing easy operation, then Claude is often the better choice. It is like a versatile writing assistant that gives you answers directly and fits well into your everyday routine, without you needing much prior knowledge. And if you tend to edit longer texts or solve creative tasks, Claude scores with its strength in handling language.

Perplexity, on the other hand, shines when you are looking for precise information on a topic and want to know where it comes from. Think of it like a search engine that not only gives you answers but also supplies the sources to go with them — ideal for research tasks or when you want to verify facts. If data protection matters to you, you should check the respective policies of both providers, as these can change and are not always specifically tailored to the DACH region. For everyday use the two tools often differ less than you might think; it really comes down to your main purpose.

FAQ

I mainly need the AI for writing and texts. Which tool is better?

For writing tasks and text generation, Claude is usually the better choice. It is designed to create, summarise and revise human-like texts, which makes it a strong partner for creative or editorial work.

I want to find information on a topic quickly and easily and see the sources for it. What is recommended?

If you are after quick information gathering with source citations, Perplexity is ideal. It works like a search engine that gives you answers directly and shows you where the information comes from, which is very useful for research work.

Are the tools free or do I have to pay for them?

Both tools usually offer free basic versions that are well suited for getting started. For advanced features, higher usage limits or special models, you need to take out a paid subscription with both providers. The exact prices and features can vary.

Is one of the tools easier to use for beginners?

Claude is often more intuitive and easier to use, especially when it comes to direct interaction and text generation. Perplexity may require a slightly better understanding of how to phrase search queries to get the best results, but it is also designed to be user-friendly.
