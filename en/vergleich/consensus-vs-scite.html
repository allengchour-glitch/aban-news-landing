Consensus vs Scite.ai: what matters when choosing

If you're looking for quick, straightforward answers to specific research questions drawn directly from scientific studies, then Consensus is often the better choice. It's like a search engine that doesn't just hand you articles but also summarises the relevant statements from them – ideal when you're short on time and need a quick overview without having to wade through entire studies. It's very intuitive to use, so you can get going right away even without prior knowledge.

If, on the other hand, you're after a deeper analysis of how often and in what context a particular study is cited, and you want to follow the arguments of other researchers, then Scite.ai is the stronger option. It shows you not only who cites whom, but also whether the citations are supporting, contradicting or merely mentioning. That's especially useful when you want to check the credibility of a claim or carry out a comprehensive literature review for an academic paper. It takes a bit more learning, but in return it offers more detailed insights.

FAQ

Which tool is better if I just need a quick overview of a topic?

For a quick overview, Consensus is usually the better choice. It extracts and summarises the key findings from studies, so you quickly get an answer to your question without having to read for ages.

I have to write an academic paper and need detailed information about citations. Which tool helps me more with that?

Scite.ai has a clear edge here. It shows you not only who cites a study, but also whether the citing study supports, contradicts or merely mentions the findings. That is crucial for a well-founded academic paper.

Are the tools understandable for non-experts too, or do I need prior knowledge of AI?

Both tools are designed so that you don't need any special prior AI knowledge. Consensus is somewhat simpler and more intuitive for getting started right away, while Scite.ai requires a short learning curve for its more advanced features.

Are there big differences in cost, or are they comparable?

The pricing models can vary. It's wise to check the current offers directly on the providers' websites, as they often offer different subscriptions for different usage intensities. Look out for free trial periods so you can try the tools out.
