Cartesia AI vs ElevenLabs: what matters when choosing

When you're choosing between Cartesia AI and ElevenLabs, it depends heavily on exactly what you have in mind. If you need a quick and uncomplicated way to turn text into speech without a long onboarding, and you value a broad choice of languages, then ElevenLabs could be the better choice for you. It's often the first port of call for many who simply need good speech output and don't want to worry about technical details.

If, on the other hand, you're looking for a solution that integrates more deeply into your existing workflows, perhaps because you need special customisations or stronger control over the finer points of voice generation, then Cartesia AI could be more interesting. Especially when it comes to data privacy in the DACH region, or when you want to clone a particular kind of voice that has to be very specific, Cartesia AI often offers more possibilities for developers and more technically savvy users. So think carefully about how important ease of use is to you versus deep customisability and integration.

FAQ

Which tool is easier to use if I've never worked with AI voices?

ElevenLabs is usually easier for beginners. The interface is more intuitively designed, so you get results quickly without having to fight your way through lots of settings. It's like a template where you just insert your text and pick a voice.

I want to clone a very specific voice. Which tool is better suited for this?

For cloning very specific voices, Cartesia AI often offers more detailed options and control. It's aimed more at developers who want to dive deeper into the matter and make fine adjustments to achieve the best possible match.

Does data privacy, especially in the German-speaking region (DACH), play a role in the choice?

Yes, data privacy can play a role. Cartesia AI often offers more options here for local integration or for specific data privacy requirements, which can be important for companies in the DACH region. With ElevenLabs you should check where the data is processed and whether that meets your requirements.

I need the tool to integrate it into an existing piece of software or application. Which is better?

Cartesia AI is usually better suited for integration into existing software solutions. It often offers more robust interfaces (APIs) that make it easier for developers to build voice generation directly into their own applications and control it.
