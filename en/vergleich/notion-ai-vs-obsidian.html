Notion AI vs Obsidian: what matters when choosing

When you are facing the choice of whether Notion AI or Obsidian suits you better, you should first think about how you want to work and what your priorities are. Notion AI is ideal if you are looking for an all-in-one solution that seamlessly combines notes, project management and support from artificial intelligence (AI). It is especially suited to teams or individuals who value ease of use, plenty of ready-made features and the ability to quickly create different types of content without having to dive deep into the technology. The AI features help you directly with writing, summarising or developing ideas, which can considerably speed up your workflow.

Obsidian, on the other hand, is the right choice if data security and full control over your notes matter most to you. Your data is stored locally on your computer, which is a big plus for data protection in the DACH region. It is perfect for knowledge workers, researchers or anyone who wants to link complex information together and build a personal knowledge network. The learning curve is a bit steeper at first, as there are fewer ready-made structures, but in return it offers enormous flexibility and customisability through plugins that you can add as you like.

FAQ

Is Notion AI or Obsidian better if I don't want to store my data in the cloud?

If it is important to you that your data stays locally on your device and is not stored in a cloud, Obsidian is the better choice. Notion AI stores your data in the cloud, which could be a data protection concern for some users.

Which tool is easier to learn if I have no experience with AI tools yet?

Notion AI is usually easier to learn, especially if you are already familiar with Notion. The AI features are integrated directly into the application and intuitive to use, often just one click away. Obsidian requires a little more familiarisation to unfold its full strength.

Can I organise and link my notes well with both tools?

Yes, both tools allow good organisation and linking of notes. Notion AI offers databases and pages for this, while Obsidian specialises in linking individual notes through links and a visual representation of your knowledge network.

Which tool is better suited for team collaboration?

Notion AI has a clear advantage when it comes to teamwork. It is designed for collaboration from the ground up and offers features such as sharing pages, comments and real-time co-editing. Obsidian is more geared towards individual use, even though it does offer synchronisation options.
