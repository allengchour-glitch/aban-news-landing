CapCut vs Topaz Video AI: what matters when choosing

When you face the choice of whether CapCut or Topaz Video AI suits you better, it depends heavily on what exactly you have in mind and how much experience you bring. CapCut is ideal for you if you want to edit videos quickly and easily, perhaps for social media. It is free, easy to use and offers many ready-made effects and templates into which you just drop your text. If data protection matters to you and you prefer a solution that runs on your computer without sending data to the cloud, CapCut — which is often associated with Chinese servers — might not be the first choice.

Topaz Video AI, on the other hand, is the right choice if you want to significantly improve the quality of your videos, for example sharpening or upscaling old footage. It is a more professional tool that you buy once and then use on your own computer, which gives you full control over your data. The operation is a little more complex and requires more learning, but the results in video optimisation are often impressive. Bear in mind that Topaz Video AI needs special hardware on your computer to work optimally, while CapCut also runs on simpler devices.

FAQ

I want to quickly create a video for TikTok or Instagram. Which tool is better?

For quick social media videos CapCut is the better choice. It is free, has many trendy effects and templates that you can use straight away, and is very easy to operate.

I have old family videos that I want to improve and sharpen. Which tool helps me with that?

For improving video quality, such as sharpening or upscaling old videos, Topaz Video AI is the clearly better choice. It is specialised in intelligently optimising videos and restoring details.

Can one of the tools be used for free?

Yes, CapCut can be used for free in its basic version, both as an app and as a desktop program. Topaz Video AI is paid software that you buy once.

Which tool is easier to learn if I have no experience with video editing yet?

CapCut is considerably easier to learn and intended for beginners. The interface is intuitive and the many templates help you achieve results quickly. Topaz Video AI requires a little more learning.
