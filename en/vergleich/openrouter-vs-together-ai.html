OpenRouter vs Together AI: what matters when choosing

You're facing the choice between OpenRouter and Together AI and wondering which service suits you better. Put simply: if you want to try out many different AI models without committing, and value flexibility, OpenRouter is often the better choice. It's like a marketplace where you can find and use many different models from various providers, which is great if you're still experimenting or have very specific requirements that aren't covered by a single provider.

Together AI, on the other hand, is ideal if you've already settled on certain powerful open-source models and want to integrate them into your applications in a stable and cost-efficient way. They offer an optimised platform for a selection of models that they host and maintain themselves. That's especially advantageous if you're looking for a reliable, high-performance solution for continuous use and the selection of models Together AI offers is enough for you. It's often the more direct and potentially cheaper option when you know exactly what you need.

FAQ

What is the biggest difference between OpenRouter and Together AI for me as a user?

OpenRouter is like a central access point to many different AI models from various providers, whereas Together AI hosts and optimises a selection of models itself. With OpenRouter you have more choice; with Together AI you often get a more direct connection to the models they offer.

When should I choose OpenRouter?

Choose OpenRouter if you want to test many different AI models to find the best one for your task, or if you need access to models that Together AI doesn't offer. It's ideal for experiments and when you need maximum flexibility in model choice.

When should I prefer Together AI?

Go for Together AI if you already know which open-source models you want to use and want to run them in a stable, fast and potentially cheaper way. They are strong when it comes to hosting and optimising specific, powerful models that they offer themselves.

Should I expect a difficult onboarding with either provider?

Both services are designed to make using AI models easier. If you've ever used an API (an interface through which programs communicate with each other), you'll find your way around quickly. For beginners, both offer good documentation to make getting started easier.
