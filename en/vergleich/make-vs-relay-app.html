Make.com vs Relay.app: what matters when choosing

You're facing the choice between Make.com and Relay.app to automate recurring tasks. Put simply: if you're after a comprehensive control centre that can connect many different apps and you're willing to invest a little time learning it, Make.com is often the better choice. It's like a Swiss army knife for your digital workflows, offering maximum flexibility, but it also demands a bit more understanding of how it works to get the most out of it. The pricing structure is often more flexible and scales with your usage, which can be an advantage at high volume.

Relay.app, on the other hand, is ideal if you want to automate typical office tasks quickly and easily, especially those involving emails, documents or calendars. It's more user-friendly and often faster to set up, as it focuses on common use cases and offers you ready-made workflows — much like a template into which you only need to drop your content. If ease of use and a quick start matter more to you and you don't necessarily need to connect exotic apps, Relay.app could be the more suitable solution for you. Bear in mind that the emphasis on DACH data protection can differ between the two tools, which matters when handling sensitive data.

FAQ

I'm a complete beginner. Which tool is easier to learn?

For complete beginners, Relay.app is usually easier. It is designed to guide you quickly through common automations and has a very intuitive interface that requires less technical knowledge. Make.com offers more possibilities, but it is also more complex and has a steeper learning curve to understand and use all the features effectively.

I want to connect many different apps, including lesser-known ones. Which tool is better?

Here Make.com has a clear advantage. It offers a huge library of integrations, i.e. connections to countless apps and services, including many niche applications. Relay.app focuses more on the most common business tools and has a smaller selection of direct connections.

I have recurring tasks that require very specific logic or conditions. Which tool is more flexible?

Make.com offers considerably more flexibility here. You can build complex workflows with many branches, conditions and custom functions. Relay.app is designed more for linear and standardised workflows and quickly reaches its limits with very specific or complex requirements.

What about data protection, especially in the DACH region?

Both providers are based outside the DACH region, which means you should check their respective data protection policies carefully. It is important to look at where the data is processed and stored and whether the tools meet GDPR requirements. A blanket statement is difficult here, as policies and certifications can change.
