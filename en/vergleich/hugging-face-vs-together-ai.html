Hugging Face vs Together AI: what matters when choosing

If you're facing the choice of whether to use Hugging Face or Together AI for your AI projects, it depends a lot on what you have in mind. Are you looking for a huge library with many different AI models that you want to customise and try out yourself? Then Hugging Face is often the better choice. It's like a big public playground for AI, where everyone can share their creations and use those of others. But if you want to use ready-made AI models quickly and easily — models that are already prepared to understand or create your texts — and you don't want to worry too much about the technical details, then Together AI might be a better fit.

Your decision also depends on how much you want to build yourself and how important ease of use is to you. Hugging Face gives you a lot of freedom and control, but also requires you to engage with the subject a bit more. It's ideal if you want to experiment and dive deeper into AI development. Together AI, on the other hand, is often easier to operate and designed to deliver results quickly without you needing to be an AI expert. It's especially useful if you want to integrate AI into existing applications without much customisation work.

FAQ

What is the main difference if I want to use AI models?

Hugging Face is like a huge marketplace for AI models, where you find many different models that you can download and run yourself. Together AI gives you direct access to selected, powerful models that you can use immediately via an interface, without having to host them yourself.

Which tool is better if I'm just starting to get into AI?

If you're just starting out and want to see results quickly, Together AI is often easier, as it offers you ready-made solutions. Hugging Face requires a bit more learning, but also gives you the chance to dive deeper into the subject and learn more.

Does data protection play a role in my decision?

With Hugging Face you have more control if you host models yourself, which can be relevant for data protection. Together AI processes your data on their servers, which means you have to trust their privacy policy. Check carefully here where the data is processed and whether that fits your use cases in Germany or the DACH region.

Which tool is more flexible if I have special requirements?

Hugging Face is more flexible if you want to customise models or develop your own, since it is an open platform. Together AI is optimised for using existing, often very good models, but offers fewer options for deep customisation.
