DeepL Write vs Grammarly: what matters when choosing

If you write texts in German and value precise, natural language improvement, then DeepL Write could be the better choice for you. It is particularly strong at optimising German phrasing and refining style, so your texts are not only grammatically correct but also sound fluent and authentic. This is ideal if you work in the German-speaking region and want to reach your audience with high-quality German content without having to worry about complicated settings.

Grammarly, on the other hand, is the first choice if you frequently write English texts or are looking for a tool that integrates seamlessly into many of your existing programs. It offers comprehensive suggestions for grammar, spelling and style in English and is known for its ease of use. If your budget is more flexible and you are looking for an all-round talent for various writing tasks that goes beyond basic corrections, then Grammarly is worth considering.

FAQ

What is the biggest difference between DeepL Write and Grammarly?

DeepL Write specialises in improving German texts and making them sound more natural. Grammarly is a comprehensive tool for English texts that also checks grammar and style.

Which tool is better for me if I only write occasionally?

For occasional use both are good in their basic functions. DeepL Write is simpler if it is only about German texts. Grammarly is more broadly positioned if you also write in English now and then.

Do I need to worry about my data when using such tools?

Both providers take data protection seriously. Make sure to read the privacy policies, especially if you are editing sensitive content. In general, you should not enter strictly confidential information into such tools.

Can I also use these tools in Word or other programs?

DeepL Write is primarily a web application into which you paste your text. Grammarly offers browser extensions and integrations with programs like Microsoft Word, which often makes it more convenient to use.
