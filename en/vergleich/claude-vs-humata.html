Claude vs Humata: what matters when choosing

When you face the choice of whether Claude or Humata fits you better, it depends a lot on what you actually intend to do. Do you need a versatile language assistant that understands, writes and summarises text, or is your main concern getting information quickly out of large amounts of your own documents? Claude is more of the all-rounder for text, while Humata is the specialist for your PDFs and other files. So think carefully about what you mainly want to use the AI for.

Another important point is how deeply you want to get into the subject and how important data protection is to you. Claude is a large language model developed by a company in the US and offers you a broad range of features you can use directly. Humata, on the other hand, focuses on analysing your own data and often places a stronger emphasis on the secure processing of your documents, which is a decisive criterion for many in the DACH region. Integration into existing workflows could also matter: do you need an API (an interface through which programs talk to each other) to embed Claude into your own applications, or is a simple interface to upload your documents and ask questions enough for you?

FAQ

When is Claude the better choice for me?

Claude is ideal if you're looking for a versatile text assistant. It can write, rewrite, and summarise text or develop creative ideas. So if you often deal with various text tasks and need an intelligent conversation partner, you're in the right place here.

When should I choose Humata?

Humata is perfect if your main goal is to get fast, precise answers from your own documents (such as PDFs or Word files). Imagine you have a stack of contracts or research papers and want to ask targeted questions about them without having to read everything yourself.

Is one of the tools easier to use if I have no prior AI experience?

Both tools are designed to be as intuitive as possible. Claude works like a chat where you simply type in your questions or instructions. Humata requires you to upload your documents first, but after that you can also ask questions about them in a chat. So both are well suited for beginners too.

What about data protection if I have sensitive documents?

With sensitive documents, you should always check carefully how the respective provider handles your data. Humata often specifically advertises features for the secure handling of company data. With Claude, your inputs are in principle used to improve the model, unless you explicitly object to this or use special enterprise solutions.
