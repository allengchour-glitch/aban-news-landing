Leonardo.ai vs Midjourney: what matters when choosing

When you're deciding whether Leonardo.ai or Midjourney suits you better, first think about what exactly you want to create and how much control you want over the process. Midjourney is often the first choice when you care about impressive, artistic images that often look very aesthetic even with short instructions. It is ideal if you want to achieve visually appealing results quickly, without having to dig deep into technical details, and if you're willing to adapt to the tool's style.

Leonardo.ai, on the other hand, offers you more options for individual customisation and control, especially if you need specific image styles or elements. It is better suited if, for example, you want to create images for a particular product, characters for a game or very specific graphics for marketing purposes. If it's also important to you that you can use your own images as a basis to alter them, or if you want to develop a particular visual language that stands out from the typical AI styles, then Leonardo.ai could be the more suitable choice for you.

FAQ

I'm completely new to AI images. Which tool is easier to learn?

Midjourney is often easier for beginners, as it works via Discord and you quickly see impressive results. Leonardo.ai does offer more features, but it can feel a bit overwhelming at first if you don't yet have any experience.

I want to create images for my business. Which tool is better for professional use?

For professional use, especially if you need specific styles or adjustments, Leonardo.ai often offers more control and flexibility. Midjourney does deliver artistic images, but adapting them to very specific brand guidelines can be a bigger challenge there.

Is one of the tools better if I want to use my own images as a template?

Yes, Leonardo.ai generally offers better and more intuitive features here for uploading your own images and using them as a basis for new creations. With Midjourney this is also possible, but often less direct and with more effort involved.

Are there differences in the pricing models that I should be aware of?

Both tools offer various subscription models based on usage intensity. It's important to check the current prices and the included features on the respective websites, as these can change and often depend on the number of images you want to create.
