Bolt.new vs Lovable: what matters when choosing

If you're facing the choice between Bolt.new and Lovable, it depends heavily on what exactly you have in mind and how much time you want to invest. Bolt.new is often the better choice if you want to get started quickly and easily, perhaps even without much prior knowledge. It's usually set up so that you can dive straight in, similar to a template where you just insert your text, and it's well suited to projects where you don't want to go deep into the technology. Even if your budget is rather small and you don't need complex customisations, Bolt.new could be the more fitting option.

Lovable, on the other hand, plays to its strengths when you need more control and customisation options and are willing to dig in a little deeper. It's often more flexible and offers more features, which makes it ideal if you have special requirements or want to connect the tool tightly with other systems. If data protection and a server location in Germany, Austria or Switzerland (the DACH region) are especially important to you, you should look closely at both, as this can vary by provider. So think carefully: do you need it simple and fast, or is deep customisability more important to you?

FAQ

What's the biggest difference between Bolt.new and Lovable for everyday use?

In everyday use the biggest difference is often complexity and customisability. Bolt.new is usually easier to operate and quicker to set up, whereas Lovable offers more options for individual design and integration, which can also mean a longer learning period.

Which tool is better if I have no idea about AI?

If you have no prior AI knowledge at all, Bolt.new is often the better choice. It's usually designed so that you can use intuitive templates or assistants that take the work off your hands and don't overwhelm you with jargon.

Does data protection play a role in the decision?

Yes, data protection is an important point. With both tools you should check carefully where the data is stored and which privacy policies apply. If server locations in the DACH region matter to you, you have to ask each provider individually, as this is not standard with all of them.

Can I connect my existing programs with either of the two tools?

That depends heavily on the respective tool. Lovable often offers more interfaces (integrations) to connect it with other programs. With Bolt.new the integration options are usually simpler or limited to common applications. Check the exact details from the providers here.
