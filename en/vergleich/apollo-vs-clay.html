Apollo.io vs Clay: what matters when choosing

If you are facing the choice between Apollo.io and Clay, a simple consideration helps: are you looking for an all-in-one solution that gives you everything from a single source to find and approach new customers? Then Apollo.io is probably the better choice for you. It is like a Swiss army knife for sales: you find contacts, can send emails and track the success of your campaigns right there. That makes it especially attractive if you want to get started quickly and prefer an integrated solution that takes many steps off your hands.

If, on the other hand, you need a tool that fits seamlessly into your existing systems and offers you maximum flexibility in searching and enriching data, then take a closer look at Clay. Clay is more of a toolkit with which you can solve very specific and complex tasks, for example when you want to merge data from many different sources. It requires a bit more learning and technical understanding, but it gives you the freedom to map exactly the processes you need for your company, and it is often the choice when you already use many other tools and want to connect them optimally.

FAQ

What is the biggest day-to-day difference if I only look for a few new contacts per week?

For simply searching for contacts, both tools are well suited. Apollo.io is often more intuitive here, as it offers a direct search function and built-in email options. With Clay you might first have to set up a small workflow to achieve the same results, which means a bit more effort.

I'm not a tech expert. Which tool is easier to learn?

Apollo.io is generally easier to learn, especially if you have no experience with such tools yet. The interface is clear and many features are self-explanatory. Clay requires a bit more learning, as it is more modular and you often have to connect different 'building blocks' to reach your goal.

Does data protection matter if I want to approach customers in Germany, Austria or Switzerland?

Yes, data protection is very important. Both tools are US providers and are therefore subject to US law. With both you should check how they handle the GDPR and what data they store. Always clarify with your data protection officer whether the use is permissible for your specific use cases.

I already use a CRM system. Which tool integrates better?

Both tools offer integrations, but in different ways. Apollo.io often has direct integrations with common CRMs. Thanks to its open and flexible architecture, Clay is very good at connecting to almost any system via APIs (interfaces for data exchange), but this often requires a bit more technical know-how.
