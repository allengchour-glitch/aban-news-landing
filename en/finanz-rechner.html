Finance Calculator for the Self-Employed

Three down-to-earth calculators for the numbers many people trip over: a realistic hourly rate, the reserve for taxes and contributions, and your buffer for lean months. Everything is calculated locally in your browser — nothing is sent or stored.

🧮 1. Realistic hourly rate

The most common mistake: dividing your desired salary by 160 hours. In reality only part of your time is billable — nobody pays you directly for acquisition, bookkeeping and breaks.

💶 2. Tax & social-contribution reserve

Set aside a fixed share of every payment immediately into a separate account. Then the tax bill won't floor you. Pick a benchmark or enter your own.

🛟 3. Cashflow buffer

How long can you hold out if no money comes in for three months? Three to six months of fixed costs in a separate account is common.
