AI Chatbots & DM Automation

When the same questions keep landing in your Instagram, WhatsApp or Messenger DMs, it eats your time. Here's the honest take on which tool automates that sensibly, where the limits are — and who it's not worth it for.

Transparency: I only recommend what I know — and name the limits. ManyChat here is a recommendation with no commission (no affiliate program active right now). If I ever add a partner link, I'll mark it clearly with *. The neutral tool database stays affiliate-free anyway.

First: is it worth it for you?

✅ Yes, if …

  • the same questions come up regularly (prices, dates, availability)
  • you actively sell or collect inquiries on IG/WhatsApp
  • you want to capture leads without being glued to your phone at night

🚫 Probably not, if …

  • only a few DMs come in per week (quick replies are enough)
  • your inquiries are individual every time
  • you'd have to process sensitive data over chat

The practical pick: ManyChat

For most solos and small shops, ManyChat is the most pragmatic entry point. It covers Instagram, WhatsApp and Messenger DMs in one builder: auto-replies, lead capture (name/email), simple sales flows, keyword triggers ("DM INFO"). No code — you drag flows together and test them live.

Where it's good: fast start, solid IG integration, usable free tier. My limit: with many contacts the paid plan adds up, and it only pays off once real volume comes in. Hand anything complex/sensitive to a human.

Honest alternatives

Native tools (free)

Instagram & WhatsApp Business have built-in quick replies, away and greeting messages. For low volume that's often perfectly enough — and free. Start here before paying for a tool.

Other platforms

There are alternatives (e.g. Chatfuel, Tidio, Landbot). I deliberately don't link them as a recommendation because I haven't used them day to day. You'll find them without affiliate bias in the neutral tool database.

Start in 4 steps

  1. Note the 5 most common questions that come in via DM.
  2. Write a short, honest auto-reply for each (AI helps — see below).
  3. Build in a clear hand-off to you ("Type HUMAN and someone gets back to you").
  4. Test for a week, then refine. Don't automate everything at once.
