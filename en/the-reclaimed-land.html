The Reclaimed Land

A North Sea saga in three volumes

For a century a dyke-builders' family clings to land it has wrested from the sea. When the grandfather, as dyke-reeve, conceals in 1906 that he left a faulty dyke spot unrepaired out of thrift — his neighbour drowns there in the storm tide, his farm gains the land — he lays a fault carried down three generations, until the granddaughter returns as a coastal engineer and the rising sea lays bare the old foreland. Written chapter by chapter with Claude Opus — three volumes, 48 chapters, free.

The three volumes

Volume 1 Reclaiming the Land Sankt Annen, 1901–1918

The young dyke-reeve Hauke Carstens leaves a faulty dyke spot unrepaired out of thrift. In the great storm tide the dyke breaks there — and his neighbour Momme Feddersen, who guards the spot, drowns. Hauke keeps silent; his farm gains the land.

The generation that founds the guilt, through storm tide, war and inflation.

Volume 2 Storm Tide Sankt Annen, 1934–1953

Hauke's son Bo becomes local farm leader under the Nazi regime and repeats his father's logic: in the February flood he sends a Polish forced labourer who saved his life to the most dangerous dyke spot — out of fear for his own position.

The generation that looks away and breaks on it — crisis, dictatorship, war, collapse.

Volume 3 Depoldering Sankt Annen, present day

The granddaughter Wiebke returns as a coastal engineer to plan a depoldering, and finds in the dyke records the truth about 1906. The rising sea lays bare the old foreland — and she must decide what a century of silence is worth.

The generation that looks and names — climate change, depoldering, a land the sea reclaims.

How it was made

“The Reclaimed Land” is a writing experiment: a plot bible fixes the world, the characters, the tone, and the arc across all three volumes; from it, Claude Opus writes the chapters one by one, true to continuity, volume by volume. This English edition is a faithful translation of the German original, “Marsch”. The code behind it is open in the project (ki-schriftsteller/).
