FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are the prices real and up to date?

Yes. Crypto prices update live in your browser via CoinGecko. Stock prices, financial news and AI sentiment are refreshed automatically several times a day from public sources (Yahoo Finance, CoinGecko, reputable RSS feeds). All sources are linked.

Is this investment advice?

No. This page contextualises publicly available data and is expressly not investment, tax or financial advice. Markets — especially crypto — are highly volatile; you can lose all of the money you invest. You make decisions at your own responsibility.

What does the „AI sentiment“ mean?

An AI summarises the current news situation per asset into a sentiment — bullish, neutral or bearish — with a short rationale. This is an experimental assessment, not a buy or sell recommendation, and can be wrong.

Which assets and currencies are shown?

Currently 22 assets across four categories: crypto (e.g. Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, XRP), stocks (e.g. NVIDIA, Apple, Microsoft, Tesla, Amazon), indices (S&P 500, Nasdaq, DAX, SMI) and commodities (gold, silver, oil). Switch the display between USD, CHF and EUR (ECB reference rates); indices are shown as points.

Does it cost anything?

No. The markets page, the converter and the savings calculator are free to use without login — right in your browser, with no tracking pixel.
