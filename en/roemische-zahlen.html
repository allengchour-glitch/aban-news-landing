Roman numeral converter

Enter a number for the Roman spelling — or enter a Roman numeral for the value. The converter detects the direction automatically (range I–MMMCMXCIX, i.e. 1–3999). All local in your browser, nothing is sent.

–

Roman numerals have no zero and no decimals; valid values are 1 to 3999. The canonical spelling is enforced (e.g. "IV", not "IIII"). Symbols: I=1, V=5, X=10, L=50, C=100, D=500, M=1000. All local, no upload.
