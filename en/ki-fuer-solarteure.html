AI for solar installers — where it really saves time

You mount the modules, pull the strings, deal with inverters and the grid connection. The paperwork gets done in the office in the evening. That is exactly where AI helps — not on the roof, but with the writing. Here is what actually works and what does not.

What this is not about

AI does not mount a module, does not size a system and does not do the grid connection. If someone tells you AI will double your turnover, walk away. What AI can do: take the office work off your hands that steals your evenings. For a solar business that is often worth more than any big promise.

1. Write quotes for PV systems faster

You know the kWp, the components and the prices. What eats time is putting it into clean wording. Give an AI chatbot the keywords for what is planned — "9.8 kWp on a tiled pitched roof, 22 modules, hybrid inverter, 10 kWh storage, wallbox prepared" — and have it build a clear quote text from that. You enter the kWp, components and prices yourself. The tool delivers the form, you deliver the numbers and the sizing.

2. Pre-draft registration and subsidy paperwork

A lot of paperwork comes with a PV system: cover letters to the grid operator, notes for the market data register, a covering letter for the subsidy. AI helps you turn that into clear texts and checklists — worded generally so you do not forget anything. You check and submit the specific data, deadlines and entries yourself. This is not legal advice, it is writing help.

3. Answer customer enquiries and appointments

Enquiries about the economics, rescheduled appointments, questions about the invoice — all polite, clear and without long pondering. You enter the key points, AI drafts a friendly reply in your tone. Especially with difficult emails (a complaint, a delayed module delivery) it helps to have a draft made first and then smooth the tone, instead of typing in anger.

4. Reply to online reviews

Replying to Google reviews brings in new customers — but who feels like coming up with replies in the evening? Enter the review and have a suitable response suggested. With criticism the rule is: stay factual, keep it short, no justifications. AI helps you hit exactly that tone instead of sounding snippy.

5. Website, social media and fitter job ad

A short description of your services for the website, a post about the finished photovoltaic installation, a job ad for the new fitter or apprentice. All texts you rarely write and that therefore take ages. AI gets you to a usable draft in five minutes that you only need to adjust.

Honest limits:
  • AI does not size a system. Sizing, electrics and structural work stay the job of a qualified professional.
  • It does not calculate correct prices. Costing stays your job.
  • You check subsidy and registration details yourself. AI does not replace legal advice.
  • It sometimes invents numbers. Read every text against the facts before it goes out.
  • Do not type sensitive customer data into free tools — the GDPR applies to you too.

Frequently asked questions

Is AI worth it for a small solar business?
Yes, if you have a lot of paperwork: quotes, registration texts, emails, review replies. That is exactly where AI saves time. For mounting, system sizing and the grid connection it changes nothing.
Can I have AI create complete quotes for PV systems?
The text part and the structure, yes. You have to enter and check kWp, components and prices yourself. AI writes the quote, it does not size the system and does not do the costing for you.
May AI handle the registration with the grid operator or the market data register?
No. AI only helps put cover letters and notes into clear wording. You check the data, deadlines and entries yourself and submit them yourself. This is not legal advice.
Is my customer data safe with AI tools?
Use tools with EU hosting or business plans with a data agreement. Do not enter full address or personal data into free consumer versions.
