How the audit works

  1. Collect questions. 10–15 real questions customers use to search for what you offer — templates by industry included.
  2. Ask the AI. Put the same questions to ChatGPT, Perplexity & Google AI and record the answers in the log.
  3. Evaluate. For each question, note: mentioned / not mentioned / competitor mentioned — that gives you your visibility status.
  4. Find the causes. Checklist: consistent data, schema markup, directories, reviews, content.
  5. 90-day plan. Prioritised actions for months 1–3, with the most important first.
  6. Re-measure. Same log after 90 days — you see in black and white what has moved.
