    How the hype filter calculates

    The text runs through an analysis engine that runs at the edge — directly in the Cloudflare function, not in your browser. It checks for four hype categories (superlatives, buzzwords, exaggerated promises, intensifiers), for filler words, passive constructions and run-on sentences.

    The plain-language score (0–100) weighs the findings and adjusts for text length — a long text with two buzzwords scores better than a short one full of hype. Readability is estimated as an approximate school level.

    Your text is only processed for the analysis and stored nowhere. No account, no tracking. The optional AI rewrite sends the text once to a language model; without a configured key you get the rule-based version instead.
