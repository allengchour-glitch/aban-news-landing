Consulting & AI Setup for DACH Solopreneurs

I'm Aban. I help solopreneurs in the DACH region put AI and automation to practical use — no buzzword bingo, just concrete workflows and an honest take on what's worth it and what isn't. You don't get an agency pitch deck; you get someone who uses these tools every day and will tell you where to start and where to save yourself the trouble.

Three ways to work with me

All prices are guide prices and can be adjusted depending on scope. Tell me briefly where you stand and we'll find the format that fits.

Strategy call (60 min)

Guide price: CHF 290

For you if you're thinking, "I have no idea where to even start with AI." We walk through your day-to-day and sort out what actually makes sense.

Output: a prioritised three-step list for your specific stack — what to tackle first, what comes later, and what you can ignore for now.

AI setup sprint (half day, remote)

Guide price: CHF 990

We build one or two real workflows together — receipt capture, lead research or content repurposing, for example. Nothing theoretical; something that runs afterwards.

Setup: built GDPR-compliant, EU hosting where possible. By the end of the half day you have working processes, not slides.

Monthly sparring

Guide price: CHF 490 / month

For anyone who wants to keep at it, instead of a one-off fireworks display followed by nothing. We hold the pace and keep adjusting your setup as tools or requirements change.

Included: two 45-minute calls per month plus async questions in between, whenever you get stuck somewhere.

Who this is NOT for

So we don't waste each other's time — here's what you won't get from me:

  • No glossy decks. You won't get a 40-page agency PDF that looks good and changes nothing. You get workflows that run.
  • No 10x promises. I'll tell you honestly when a tool does little for your case — even when that sounds less like a sale.
  • No "AI solves everything." Some things are better left un-automated. If that's the case for you, I'll say so rather than sell you something anyway.
  • No full outsourcing. I build with you, not for you behind the scenes. Afterwards you should understand what's running and be able to adjust it yourself.

If that sounds too sober for you, I'm probably the wrong choice — and that's completely fine.
