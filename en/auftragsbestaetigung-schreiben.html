What goes in

The basics

Your details and the client's, an order/confirmation number, the date and the reference to the quote — so everything is clearly traceable.

Scope & price

Exactly what you deliver and at what price. The more concrete, the fewer follow-up questions later.

Deadline & terms

The delivery or completion date and the payment terms. Clear dates protect both sides.

Common questions

What is an order confirmation?

You confirm in writing that you accept the order and record scope, price, deadline and terms — clarity, fewer misunderstandings.

Quote or order confirmation?

The quote comes before (your proposal), the confirmation after (acceptance). Often this forms the contract.

What goes in?

Your + client details, order number + date, quote reference, scope, price, deadline, payment terms. Not legal advice.
