Compute SHA hash

SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-384 and SHA-512 of any text. For checksums, webhook signatures or to check whether two contents are identical. Local in your browser, nothing is sent.

Computed with the browser's SubtleCrypto API (UTF-8). SHA-1 is considered cryptographically broken — use SHA-256 or higher for signatures, SHA-1 only for simple checksums. Everything local, no upload. MD5 is deliberately missing (not part of the browser standard and insecure anyway).
