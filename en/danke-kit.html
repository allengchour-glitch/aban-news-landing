New to ZIP files? Start here.
  1. Download: Once the payment check confirms your download is ready, select “Download kit” above. A ZIP file holds the files in your package.
  2. Find the file: Look in your browser's downloads or the “Downloads” folder. On a phone, try the “Files” or “My Files” app.
  3. Extract: Open the ZIP file and choose “Extract” or “Extract all”. On some devices, double-clicking or tapping is enough. Then open the resulting folder.
  4. Read the included instructions: Packages contain different formats and have different requirements. Start with your package's instructions before opening its other files.
