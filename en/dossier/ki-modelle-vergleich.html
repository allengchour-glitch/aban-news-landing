Claude, Mistral, Aleph Alpha & co. — who really does what.

In depth: choosing a model without falling for the benchmark circus

Which AI model is "the best" seems to change weekly — and that's the trap. Leaderboards measure lab conditions, not your inbox. The better question isn't "which is on top", but "which fits my task, my budget and my data".

What actually matters when choosing

  • Fit for your task: test with your real texts/data. A model that tops the benchmark can still be worse for your use case.
  • Where the data sits: EU providers like Mistral or Aleph Alpha are often the stronger argument in the DACH region — not because of hype, but because of processing location and compliance.
  • Cost & commoditisation: the price war (also from China) increasingly turns language models into a commodity. Don't pay for a name, pay for measurable value.
  • Lock-in: the easier you can switch provider, the more calmly you'll ride out the next market shift.

Why "the market leader" is rarely the right answer

The gap between the top models is shrinking, while specialisation and price differ more. For most tasks "good enough" is plenty — and "good enough, EU-hosted, half the price" beats "top of the chart with open data-protection questions".

Rule of thumb: pick two candidates, test them for a week on real work, decide by result and data protection — not by ranking.

Frequently asked questions

Which AI model is the best?

There's no single "best" for everyone. For your specific task a short practical test with real data decides — plus data protection and cost. The gap between the top models is smaller than the headlines suggest.

Are EU providers like Mistral or Aleph Alpha a real alternative?

For many DACH applications, yes — mainly because of processing location and compliance. On raw performance it depends on the use case, so test yourself rather than trusting the ranking.

Is it worth switching models all the time?

No. Choose by fit and keep the switching effort (lock-in) small. Only switch when it noticeably improves value or data protection.
