4. Voice & Tone

aban news sounds like a Swiss/German friend who uses AI every day and won't tolerate any bullshit. Directly respectful, no marketing speak, with a state-of-play briefing.

Voice pillars

  • Concrete: numbers, examples, real use cases instead of buzzwords
  • Direct: say what's what, without filler or politeness bloat
  • Respectful: the reader is treated as competent, not lectured
  • Self-critical: our own mistakes and limitations are named
  • Helpful: every issue must deliver at least ONE actionable point

Do

  • “Claude Sonnet 4.7 costs $3 per 1M input tokens. Here's a prompt that saves me 30 min:"
  • “Make.com used 600 operations in 3 months — the free tier is enough for a DACH solo operator."
  • “This method works for me. It might not for you. Test it."
  • “Here's my mistake from last week, and what I learned from it."
  • “Not in the mood for 47 newsletters? I get it. Subscribe anyway, or don't."

Don't

  • “Revolutionise your productivity with our cutting-edge AI-powered workflow!"
  • “AI is the future — if you don't act now, you'll get left behind!"
  • “These 7 hacks will 10x transform your business!"
  • “I'm not allowed to reveal what ChatGPT can REALLY do…"
  • “Hi, my name is Aban, and I help solopreneurs to…"

5. Photography & Iconography

aban news uses NO stock photos. No generic AI images of blue brains or floating robot hands. Instead: inline SVGs, emojis, clear typographic accents.

Rules

  • Icons: inline SVG, min. 24px, max. 64px for UI; use currentColor
  • Emojis: system emojis preferred (no custom emoji set) — ☕ ⚡ 🔍 💡 ⚠️
  • Illustrations: if needed, then minimal, monochrome, in brand amber
  • Photos: only own photos (by the author) or explicitly licensed custom shots
  • No AI image without labelling: if used, then with the label “AI-generated"

8. Brand promises

7 core promises that must be kept in every aban news touchpoint.

1. No hype

We don't sell AI bullshit. If something sounds hypey, we rewrite it — or leave it out.

2. 3-5 minutes

Every newsletter issue is read in 3-5 min. Longer = we haven't curated enough.

3. GDPR-first

No tracking cookie without opt-in. No marketing pixel. Plausible instead of GA.

4. Personally curated

Every issue is checked by a human (Aban) before it goes out. AI assists. The human decides.

5. Sources linked

Every claim → a primary source. No second-hand. No “a study shows…"

6. At least 1 actionable

Every issue has at least ONE thing readers can apply today.

7. Corrections visible

When we make mistakes, we correct them publicly in the next newsletter with a date.

9. Don'ts — what destroys brand consistency

This list is non-negotiable. Any element that violates one of these points is rejected — no matter how nice it looks or how much engagement it brings.

  • No marketing clichés: “game-changer", “revolutionary", “cutting-edge", “synergy"
  • No affiliate links without labelling: if used, clearly declared
  • No stock photos: never. Full stop.
  • No generic AI images: no blue brain, no floating hand
  • No clickbait: “You won't believe what Claude can do now…"
  • No FOMO tactics: “only 24 hrs left", “only the first 100 subs"
  • No tracking pixel: in the newsletter or on the site
  • No pop-ups: never. Not even “exit-intent".
  • No buzzword-bingo headlines: “AI-powered, blockchain-enabled, web3-native"
  • No attacks on competitors: we talk about ourselves, not against others
