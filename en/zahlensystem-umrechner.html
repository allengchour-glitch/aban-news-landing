Convert number systems

Convert binary, octal, decimal and hexadecimal into each other — type in any field, the others update automatically. Works precisely even for very large numbers (BigInt). All local in your browser, nothing is sent.

Only the digits of the respective system are allowed (hex also a–f, upper/lower case does not matter). On an invalid entry the field is marked and the others stay put. Whole, non-negative numbers are converted. All local, no upload.
