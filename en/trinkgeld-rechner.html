Tip & split

Split the bill fairly: enter the amount and tip, share it among everyone at the table — you see the tip, total and the amount per person instantly. Everything local in your browser, nothing is sent.

The tip is added to the bill amount (total = bill + tip). "Round up" rounds the amount per person up to the next franc — the sum may then be slightly above the total (a small buffer for the tip). In Switzerland tipping is voluntary; people often simply round up. Everything local, no upload.
