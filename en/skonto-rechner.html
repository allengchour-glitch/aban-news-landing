Effective annual rate

Effective annual rate = discount / (100 − discount) × 360 / (payment term − discount period). If it exceeds your overdraft/credit rate, taking the discount almost always pays off — even by pre-financing with credit. Calculated on a 360-day year. Not tax/financial advice. All local, no upload.
