Text Tool

Tidy up text fast: change UPPER/lowercase, clean up spaces, sort lines, remove duplicates or line breaks — all with one click. The steps can be chained, and “Undo” reverts the last one. Everything runs locally in your browser, nothing is sent.

“Clean up spaces” removes leading/trailing spaces on each line and collapses multiple spaces into one. Sorting & duplicates work line by line (sorting uses English sort order). Everything happens locally, nothing is uploaded.
