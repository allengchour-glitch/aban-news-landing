Guide: how to use the AI tool

Step by step, in plain language — even if you have never done anything with "AI" before. All you need is a phone or computer with internet.

For absolute beginners No jargon With an example Free
In one sentence: "AI" is just a computer helper that writes a first text for you. You roughly say what it is about — it makes the suggestion. You decide in the end what to keep. That's all you need to know.

The 3 steps

1

Tell it what you do (your industry)

At the top of the tool there is a selection field. Type or pick what your business is — for example bakery, hairdresser, workshop or shop. This way the helper knows what style to write in.

💡 Can't find your exact industry? Pick the closest one. That's perfectly fine.

2

Type a few keywords

Keywords are single words instead of full sentences. You don't have to form a nice sentence — a few words are enough, separated by commas. For example: butter braid, fresh, Sunday, handmade.

Then you also pick what you need (e.g. an ad text, a post for Instagram, or a reply to a review) and what tone it should have (friendly, casual or formal).

💡 The more specific the keywords, the better the suggestion. But don't worry — you can't do anything wrong.

3

Hit "Create" — done

Press the button. A finished text suggestion (a draft) appears instantly. Don't like it? Hit "New variant" for a different suggestion. You can also just overwrite the text yourself.

When it fits, hit "Copy text". That takes the text with you — you can then paste it somewhere else (tap/hold → "Paste"), for example on your website, on Instagram or in an email.

Example to follow along

🟢 Here's what a real run looks like — a bakery
What you type in

Industry: bakery · What you need: ad text · Keywords: butter braid, fresh, Sunday, handmade

What comes out

"Our handmade butter braid — fresh from the oven every Sunday. Baked golden brown, light and buttery. Bring a little Sunday feeling into your kitchen."

You can now change this text however you like and copy it with one click. That's it — no expert knowledge needed.

What the buttons mean

"Create"
Turns your keywords into a finished text.
"New variant"
Makes a different suggestion on the same topic — press as often as you like until one fits.
"Copy text"
Takes the text "out" so you can paste it elsewhere.
"AI roadmap"
A second area: you answer 3 short questions and get a personal plan for where AI saves you the most time in your business.

Words explained simply

AI (artificial intelligence)
A computer helper that can write texts.
Draft
A first suggestion, not finished yet — you may change it.
Template
A ready-made text pattern your keywords are placed into.
Keywords
Single words instead of full sentences, e.g. fresh, Sunday, handmade.
Tone
How the text should sound — friendly, casual or formal.
Copy
"Take out" the text to paste it somewhere else.
Hashtag (#)
A keyword with a hash sign (e.g. #bakery) — people find you more easily on Instagram & co.

If something doesn't work

No text appears when I press "Create"

Check that you picked an industry and entered a few keywords — the tool needs both. If needed, reload the page (close it and open it again).

The text doesn't fit my business

Hit "New variant" for a different suggestion, or enter more specific keywords. You can also overwrite the text yourself anytime — it's just a starting point.
