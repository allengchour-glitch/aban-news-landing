Best email marketing tools

Email is the one channel that's truly yours — no platform in between. Here's the honest take on which newsletter/email tool is worth it for solos and small businesses, with a GDPR eye and real limits.

Quick: which fits you?

✅ Start simple

  • MailerLite — clean, fast to learn, great for a first list
  • Brevo — EU-hosted, GDPR-friendly, generous free tier

🚀 More automation

  • GetResponse — funnels, landing pages, automations in one
  • (Alternative: ActiveCampaign — powerful, but pricier/more complex)

Brevo · EU-hosted, GDPR-friendly

Formerly Sendinblue, hosted in the EU — which makes the GDPR part easier. Newsletters, automations, contacts, even SMS/transactional email. Generous free entry (daily limit), paid plans by send volume rather than list size.

My limit: the interface is functional, not the prettiest; very polished funnels are a touch more elegant elsewhere.

MailerLite · simplest start

Clear and quickly understood — ideal if you're starting your first list now. Nice editor, solid automations, fair free tier. A good pick for creators and solo businesses without a tech itch.

My limit: for very complex branching/tagging strategies it eventually gets too simple — then look toward GetResponse/ActiveCampaign.

GetResponse · automation & funnels

More than newsletters: automations, landing pages, funnels, webinars under one roof. Useful when you don't just send emails but want to build whole flows (lead → sequence → offer).

My limit: more features = more to learn; for a plain, simple newsletter it's a bit much.

What to actually check

  • GDPR & server location: EU-hosted (e.g. Brevo) makes the DPA & data transfer easier.
  • Double opt-in & unsubscribe: mandatory — the tool must do it cleanly.
  • Pricing as you grow: by list size or by send volume? Estimate with your realistic list size.
  • Deliverability: more important than any extra feature. Authenticate your own domain (SPF/DKIM).
