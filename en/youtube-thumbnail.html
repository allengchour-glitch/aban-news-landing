YouTube thumbnail downloader

Paste a video link or video ID — and view the preview image in every resolution (up to HD 1280×720), open it or copy the image URL. The images come straight from the YouTube CDN; your input never leaves the browser.

"Open" shows the image in a new tab — there, right-click → "Save image as". A direct download isn't possible client-side because the image comes from YouTube's server (different origin). Not every video has the HD variant (1280×720) — then it shows "not available". All local, no upload, no API.
