AI for glass and building cleaning — where it really saves time

You clean windows, glass façades and conservatories, you work on scaffolding, an aerial platform or a ladder. The office work gets done in the evening. That is exactly where AI helps — not at the glass, but with the writing and planning. Here is what actually works and what does not.

What this is not about

AI does not clean a window, does not clean a glass façade and does not secure access at height. If someone tells you AI will double your turnover, walk away. What AI can do: take the office work off your hands that steals your evenings — prepare quotes, order routes, write emails and reviews. For a cleaning business that is often worth more than any big promise.

1. Prepare quotes and site costing

You visited the site and noted the measurements and glass areas. What eats time is putting the quote into clean wording. Give an AI chatbot the keywords for what is coming up — "office building, 60 windows both sides, glass roof conservatory, quarterly cleaning, aerial platform needed" — and have it build a clear quote text from that. You enter and check the square metres, quantities and prices yourself. The tool delivers the form, you deliver the numbers from the site visit.

2. Order route and job planning

Several sites a day, spread across the region, with different intervals. AI helps you sort a rough order when you give it the addresses, time windows and workloads as a list. It does not replace routing software with live traffic, but it quickly brings structure to a chaotic weekly list that you then fine-tune yourself. It is also handy for briefing a stand-in clearly.

3. Enquiry emails and customer communication

Enquiries, appointment confirmations, questions about the invoice — all polite, clear and without long pondering. You enter the key points, AI drafts a friendly reply in your tone. Especially with difficult emails (a complaint, streaks on the glass, a cancelled appointment) it helps to have a draft made first and then smooth the tone, instead of typing in anger.

4. Draft maintenance contracts and reminders

Many sites run on recurring cleaning — a quarterly glass façade, monthly upkeep cleaning of the glass surfaces in the entrance area. AI drafts the contract texts and the reminder for the next date in clear sentences; you provide the interval, the service and the conditions. That way you forget no follow-up cleaning and the letter looks clean every time. You or your lawyer check the legal clauses yourself.

5. Reply to online reviews

Replying to Google reviews brings in new customers — but who feels like coming up with replies in the evening? Enter the review and have a suitable response suggested. With criticism the rule is: stay factual, keep it short, no justifications. AI helps you hit exactly that tone instead of sounding snippy.

Honest limits:
  • AI does not replace a site visit or measurement on site. You measure the glass area yourself.
  • It delivers no binding calculation of the square metres or the price. That stays your job.
  • It does not assess work safety or access at height with any guarantee. Scaffolding, aerial platform and fall protection you assess yourself to the applicable rules.
  • It cleans nothing — no window, no façade, no conservatory.
  • Do not type sensitive customer or site data into free tools — the GDPR applies to you too.

Frequently asked questions

Is AI worth it for a small glass and building cleaning business?
Yes, if you have a lot of office work: quotes, enquiry emails, route planning, review replies. That is exactly where AI saves time. For the cleaning, the glass and the work at height it changes nothing.
Can AI calculate the glass area and the price for a site?
No, not bindingly. AI can help you turn notes from the site visit into clean quote wording. You have to work out and check the measurement, the square metres and the price yourself.
May AI assess work safety or access at height?
No. Scaffolding, aerial platform, rope access and fall protection you assess yourself on site to the applicable rules. That is your responsibility, AI gives no guarantee for it.
Are my customer and site data safe with AI tools?
Use tools with EU hosting or business plans with a data agreement. Do not enter full addresses, site plans or personal data into free consumer versions.
