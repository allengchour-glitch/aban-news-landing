    What the check tests — and what AEO means

    AEO stands for answer-engine optimisation: the question of how well a page can be read and cited by answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews). These systems prefer clear structure and direct statements — unlike classic search, which leans more on links and keywords.

    The check runs at the edge in a Cloudflare function and scores seven areas: answer-first structure, heading hierarchy, FAQ, structured data (JSON-LD), lists and tables, plain language and readability, plus meta information. Each area awards points, and the total makes up the score from 0 to 100.

    The tool does not query any answer engine live — it only analyses the content you paste. Your text is processed for the analysis and stored nowhere. No account, no tracking. The full HTML source is the most meaningful input, because plain text without headings and schema delivers fewer signals.
