generate robots.txt

Build crawler rules, sitemap and crawl-delay with clicks and create a valid robots.txt — to copy or as a file. Place it in your website's root directory (/robots.txt). Everything local in your browser, nothing is sent.

Paths begin with / (e.g. /admin/). An empty Disallow: line = everything allowed. robots.txt is only a request to reputable crawlers, not access protection — sensitive pages belong behind a login. Check the result in the Search Console robots.txt tester. All local, no upload.
