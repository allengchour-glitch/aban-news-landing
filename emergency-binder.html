Why this one?

  • Built-in security principle: no passwords or PINs are ever written down — only where access lives. The binder helps without becoming a risk itself.
  • Real form fields: fill it in on screen (any PDF reader) and save — or print it and write by hand.
  • Made for expat life: residence & work permits, embassy contact, accounts back home, EU succession-regulation hint — the things generic emergency binders skip.
  • Honest, no scare tactics: clear instructions, 15 minutes per chapter, “done beats perfect”. Not an 80-page monster that never gets filled in.

Honesty note: the binder organises information — it is not a legal document and no substitute for a will, living will or power of attorney. For those it points you to the official bodies of your country.
