## Product fidelity
Dress: provisional pass. Full straps and hem remain inside the portrait frame. The fitted bodice, neckline, waist seam and skirt silhouette are recognizable. Enlarged comparison retains the conspicuous blue flowers and red/orange stems in the expected bodice and upper-skirt positions. Base color remains close to the reference's warm ivory/apricot appearance. No obvious replacement floral layout, missing strap, detached garment part or gross shape change was seen. Fine fabric texture and exact seam-level identity are not pixel-certified.

Gallery: provisional pass. The front-left vessel retains its rounded body, lid and brown knob, white handles/front detail, brown rim bands and recognizable blue flower pattern. The loose foreground lid remains separate and stationary in the inspected views. Stacked jars and the already partial right-hand vessel remain consistent with the source composition. No lid opening, pouring, invented hand, new action, or reconstruction of an unseen full right-hand jar was observed.

Atelier: visual continuity pass in sampled images. Existing silk loop, amber orb, metal plinth and arched setting remain recognizable. The portrait composition crops the original wide source substantially. No new object was apparent.

## Motion and editorial quality
The warm atelier, uncluttered full dress and ceramic still life form a coherent restrained editorial sequence. Product visibility and stable composition are strengths. Cuts are clean; the bright dress makes a noticeable tonal jump from the dark atelier.

Motion is modest. In the decoded dress samples at 4 and 7 seconds, a single similarity transform explains 96.2% of 1,565 matched image features: scale 1.06473, with median inlier residual 0.624 pixels at 1000-pixel image width. In gallery samples at 7.5 and 10.5 seconds, 97.5% of 2,781 matches fit scale 1.03123, residual 0.344 pixels. These endpoint measurements support predominantly global zoom/translation in the product beats, not substantial independent product motion. They are not a complete temporal motion audit. The atelier shows subtle silk/light/composition variation in the contact samples; no strong action is established.

Suitable as a calm atmospheric website montage, with a medium-strength film impression and strong still-image art direction. It does not demonstrate the depth, dynamic parallax or tactile product motion that would justify calling it a transformative cinematic upgrade. This verdict evaluates an editorial brand film, not a performance advertisement; absence of aggressive hooks, captions and CTAs is not a defect here.

## Limits and release recommendation
No obvious product-fidelity blocker was found in inspected views. The main quality limitation is restrained, predominantly still-image motion. The 3:4 canvas also needs a portrait-safe display or deliberate responsive framing: automatic landscape cover-cropping could remove dress straps/hem and gallery edges. Seamless looping and actual playback in the website placement were not verified by this reviewer.

Keep the current approved R272 binding unchanged pending user review of this candidate. No new generation, theme mutation or publishing was performed by this reviewer.
