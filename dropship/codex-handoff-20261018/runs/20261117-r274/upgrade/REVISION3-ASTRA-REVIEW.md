## Motion and improvement over revision 2
Revision 3 gives the sequence more editorial variety through two purposeful detail cuts. The gallery full shot visibly changes from softer focus to a sharper jar composition. Dress-detail and especially jar-detail shots show changing illumination/reflections while their forms remain stable. These are meaningful motion/optical effects beyond a uniform zoom.

The full dress is now effectively camera-locked: samples at 2.5 and 4.5 seconds fit global scale 0.99809 (approximately -0.19%), versus revision 2's +6.47% across its inspected dress interval. Of 1,656 matched features, 82.85% fit the global similarity transform. Median lower-half residual is 1.22 pixels versus upper-half 0.36 pixels at 900-pixel width, consistent with small localized skirt/fold changes rather than whole-image enlargement. This supports subtle motion, not a large verified cloth flutter.

Dress-detail geometry is almost fixed (scale 0.99992; 98.85% inliers), fitting the intended light-led beat. Gallery-detail geometry is likewise stable (scale 0.99978; 96.62% inliers), while illumination visibly changes. Gallery focus change leaves fewer comparable features, so its transform statistics are less diagnostic. Final atelier samples show lateral displacement and subtle silk/composition variation; a strong physical wave or convincing deep parallax is not established by this sampling.

These endpoint feature matches are supporting evidence, not proof of physically correct dynamics or a complete motion audit.
